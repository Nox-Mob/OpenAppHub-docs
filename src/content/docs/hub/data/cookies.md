OpenAppHub and its apps use a few cookies, all set by us. No advertising or tracking cookies.

| Kind            | Cookies                                                                       | Can you turn them off?               |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| **Required**    | your sign-in session, the organization you're working in, your cookie choices | No: the apps can't work without them |
| **Preferences** | your light, dark or system theme                                              | Yes: only used if you say yes        |

Change your choices at any time: select **Cookie settings** at the bottom of any page, or in your account menu
in the apps, then **Save my choices**. One choice covers every OpenAppHub app. The full list is on the
[cookie policy](https://openapphub.org/cookies).
