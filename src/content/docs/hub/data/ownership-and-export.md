---
title: "Your data and exports"
description: "Your data stays yours, including after a plan ends."
sidebar:
  order: 1
---

Your organization's data belongs to your organization.

- **Owners and admins can export everything** from each app, at any time.
- **If a plan or trial ends, nothing is deleted.** The apps become read-only: you can still see and export
  everything until you choose a plan.
- **Each organization's data is kept separate** by the database itself, and important changes are recorded.
- **Deleting an organization** deletes its data in every app. Only the owner can do it, after typing the
  organization's name.

How to export from each app: see that app's docs.
