Start from the job you're trying to get done.

| If you need to… | Use |
|---|---|
| Keep a membership roster, collect dues online, issue receipts | [OpenCharterApp](/charter/) |
| Run a national association whose chapters each have their own members and money | [OpenCharterApp](/charter/) |
| Track who holds which officer seat and when their term ends | OpenCharterApp (for an association's officers) or OpenBoardApp (for a board) |
| Schedule board meetings, build agendas and board packets | [OpenBoardApp](/board/) |
| Hold votes during a meeting and keep an official record of them | [OpenBoardApp](/board/) |
| Write, approve and keep minutes | [OpenBoardApp](/board/) |
| Keep the books: income, expenses, bank and card accounts | [OpenLedgerApp](/ledger/) |
| Check your records against a bank statement | [OpenLedgerApp](/ledger/) |
| Track restricted gifts, pledges and budgets for a nonprofit | [OpenLedgerApp](/ledger/) |
| Hand the board a financial report each month | OpenLedgerApp's board package, attached to an OpenBoardApp meeting |

## Common combinations

- **A small association:** OpenCharterApp for members and dues, OpenLedgerApp for the books.
- **A nonprofit board:** OpenBoardApp for meetings, OpenLedgerApp for the treasurer.
- **Everything:** all three. The treasurer exports OpenCharterApp's payments for bookkeeping, keeps the books in OpenLedgerApp, and brings its board package to OpenBoardApp meetings.

## Using more than one app

Today each app has its own sign-in and its own organizations, so you create your organization in each app you use. See [How the apps fit together](/hub/start/how-the-apps-fit/) for what's planned.
