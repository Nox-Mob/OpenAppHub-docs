OpenAppHub is the front door to the suite. It holds the things every app shares:

| Shared in OpenAppHub  | What it means for you                                           |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Your account**      | One email and password (or Google) for every app.               |
| **Your organization** | Its name, its people and their roles are the same in every app. |
| **Your plan**         | One plan covers the apps your organization uses.                |

Each app keeps its own work: OpenCharterApp keeps your members and dues, OpenBoardApp your meetings and
minutes, OpenLedgerApp your books. Apps only show each other's information where they're built to.

The apps are moving onto OpenAppHub one at a time. Until an app has moved, you sign in to it separately.

:::
