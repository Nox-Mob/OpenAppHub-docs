## Today

Each app is separate:

- **Separate sign-in.** An account in one app doesn't sign you in to another.
- **Separate organizations.** Create your organization in each app you use, and invite people in each one.
- **Separate roles.** Each app has its own roles, because each job is different. A treasurer in OpenLedgerApp doesn't automatically have any role in OpenBoardApp.
- **Moving information between them** is done with exports. For example, OpenCharterApp's payments export is built for bookkeeping, and OpenLedgerApp's board package is a PDF you can attach to a board meeting.

## Planned

:::note[Not available yet]
The features below are planned. They aren't in the apps yet, and the details may change.
:::

- **One account for the whole suite.** Sign in once at OpenAppHub and open any app without signing in again.
- **One organization across apps.** Your organization and its people are set up once and shared by every app you use.
- **Links between apps.** For example, a board decision in OpenBoardApp linked to the budget it approved in OpenLedgerApp.
- **Hosted plans** that include one app or the whole suite, priced per organization rather than per person.

Every app will keep working on its own, so self-hosting a single app stays possible.
