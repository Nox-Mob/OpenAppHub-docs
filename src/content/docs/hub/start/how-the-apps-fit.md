OpenAppHub is the front door. Behind it are three apps, each made for one job:

- **OpenCharterApp** for your members.
- **OpenBoardApp** for your board.
- **OpenLedgerApp** for your money.

You don't have to use all three. Use the ones you need.

## What's shared

OpenAppHub holds the things every app shares, so you only set them up once:

| Shared in OpenAppHub             | What it means for you                                                                                                     |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Your account**                 | One email and password (or Google) works in every app. Two-step sign-in, if you turn it on, works everywhere too.         |
| **Your organization**            | Its name, its people and their roles are the same in every app. Invite someone once, and they can use the apps you allow. |
| **Your plan**                    | One plan pays for the apps your organization uses.                                                                        |
| **Your notifications and tasks** | One bell, one dashboard and one task summary email for everything.                                                        |

## What each app keeps

Each app keeps its own work:

- **OpenCharterApp** keeps your members, chapters, dues and receipts.
- **OpenBoardApp** keeps your meetings, agendas, votes and minutes.
- **OpenLedgerApp** keeps your books.

A person can have a different role in each app. For example, your treasurer might be an admin in OpenLedgerApp but only a member in OpenBoardApp. See [Organizations and roles](/hub/accounts/organizations-and-roles/).

## Where the apps work together

The apps share information only in the places they're built to. For example:

- **Officers** chosen in OpenCharterApp show up as board members in OpenBoardApp.
- **Dues** paid in OpenCharterApp can go into your books in OpenLedgerApp, after your treasurer reviews them.
- **Reports** from OpenCharterApp and OpenLedgerApp can be added to a board meeting's packet in OpenBoardApp.
- **Tasks** from every app show up on your OpenAppHub dashboard.
- **Workflows** you build in OpenAppHub can use all three apps.

The full list is in [Getting started](/hub/start/getting-started/) and each app's docs.
