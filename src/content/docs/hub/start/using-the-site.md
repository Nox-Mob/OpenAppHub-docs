## Light and dark mode

Use the theme switch at the top of the page: **Light**, **Dark** or **System**. **System** follows your
device and is the default. If you allow preference cookies, your choice is remembered across visits and
across the OpenAppHub apps; otherwise it lasts until you close the browser.

## Cookies

The first time you visit, a banner explains the cookies we use. **Accept all** and **Reject non-essential**
are both one click; **Choose** lets you decide category by category. You can change your mind at any time
with **Cookie settings** at the bottom of the page. Details: [Cookies](/hub/data/cookies/).
