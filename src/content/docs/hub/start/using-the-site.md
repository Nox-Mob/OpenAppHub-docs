This page covers the small things that work the same way in every app.

## Getting around

- **The app switcher** at the top lets you jump between OpenAppHub, OpenCharterApp, OpenBoardApp and OpenLedgerApp. You stay signed in, and the same organization stays open.
- **The organization switcher** lets you change organizations, if you belong to more than one.
- **The bell** shows your [notifications](/hub/accounts/notifications/).
- **Help links**, marked with a question mark, open the page of these docs that explains what you're looking at.

## Light and dark mode

Use the theme switch at the top of the page. There are three choices:

- **Light:** dark text on a light background.
- **Dark:** light text on a dark background. Some people find it easier on the eyes at night.
- **System:** follows your device's setting. This is the default.

If you allow preference cookies, your choice is remembered next time, and in every app. If you don't, it lasts until you close your browser.

## Cookies

Cookies are small files a website keeps in your browser. The first time you visit, a banner explains the cookies we use. You choose with one click:

- **Accept all**
- **Reject non-essential:** only the cookies the site can't work without, like the one that keeps you signed in.
- **Choose:** decide one kind at a time.

You can change your mind at any time with **Cookie settings** at the bottom of the page. The details are in [Cookies](/hub/data/cookies/).

## Staying safe

- Sign out when you're done on a shared computer.
- Turn on [two-step sign-in](/hub/accounts/two-step-sign-in/).
- Check the address bar. Our sites end in **openapphub.org**.

More tips are in [Security and privacy](/hub/help/security/).
