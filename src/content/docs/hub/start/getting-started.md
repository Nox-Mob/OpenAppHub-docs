Setting up takes a few minutes. You'll need an email address and the name of your organization.

## 1. Create your account

On [openapphub.org](https://openapphub.org), select **Start free trial**, then fill in **Your name**,
**Email** and a **Password**. Or select **Continue with Google**. Open the confirmation link we email you.
No email? See [Signing in](/hub/accounts/signing-in/#no-confirmation-email).

## 2. Follow the guided setup

After you confirm your email, OpenAppHub walks you through four steps. You can change everything later.

1. **Your organization.** Enter its name (for example *Riverside Community Association*). You become its
   **owner**.
2. **Time zone and currency.** We guess both from your browser. Every app shows your organization's dates
   in this time zone and its money in this currency. The preview shows how they'll look.
3. **Invite your team.** Add the people who run the organization with you (board members, staff or
   volunteers), one email address at a time, each with a role. You can skip this step.
   [How invitations work](/hub/accounts/invitations/).
4. **Open your apps.** OpenCharterApp, OpenBoardApp and OpenLedgerApp all use the same account and
   organization, so you won't sign in again.

:::tip[Dues-paying members]
Members of an association who pay dues are added in OpenCharterApp, not invited to OpenAppHub. Invite
only the people who help run things.
:::

## 3. Finish the checklist

Until you're done, the dashboard shows **Get set up** with what's left:

- Set your time zone and currency.
- Invite the people who run it with you.
- Choose where invoices go (optional): a treasurer's email address, if it isn't yours.

Select a step to do it. **Hide** removes the list once you don't need it.

## Joined someone else's organization?

If you accepted an invitation, there's nothing to set up: open the apps from the dashboard. Only owners and
admins see the checklist.
