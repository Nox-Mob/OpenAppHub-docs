After you sign in, the dashboard shows the organization you have open, with what matters from each app it uses.
An app you don't use leaves its panels out.

## Choose what shows

Select **Choose panels** at the top and tick the panels you want: **This week**, **Your tasks**, **Money this year**, **Upcoming meetings**, **Recent decisions**, **Officers** and **Your apps**. It's saved for you (nobody else's dashboard changes) and applies to every organization you open. Select **Done** to close it.

If you belong to several organizations, choose which one opens first on your [account page](/hub/accounts/account-settings/#your-organizations).

## At a glance

Four tiles across the top: **open tasks**, the **next meeting**, **current members** and **net this year**
(income minus spending).

## This week

The next seven days, day by day: the board meetings and everything due, from every app, with anything already overdue at the top. Days with nothing on them are left out. "Today" follows your organization's time zone.

## Your tasks

Open work from every app, soonest first. Anything past its date is shown in red. Select a task to open it in its app.

Some tasks can be finished right here:

- **Done** on an OpenBoardApp action item marks it done.
- **Skip** on an OpenLedgerApp recurring entry skips it this time (you're asked first): nothing is posted, and it comes back next time it's due.
- **Done** on a task a workflow gave you ([Automations](/hub/automations/)) marks it finished.

The app's own rules still apply, so you only see these buttons where you're allowed to use them. Tasks that mean real work, like reviewing a join request or reconciling a month, open in their app.

- **OpenBoardApp:** action items assigned to you.
- **OpenCharterApp:** join requests to review, required board seats that are empty, and renewals coming due.
- **OpenLedgerApp:** recurring entries due within a week.
- **Workflows:** tasks your organization's workflows gave you, and **approvals waiting for you** (*An award submission from Pat Lee: pending your approval*). An approval opens its page, where you approve or decline ([Approvals](/hub/automations/approvals/)).

## The rest of the page

- **Money this year:** income and spending by month, from OpenLedgerApp.
- **Upcoming meetings** and **recent decisions** (motions passed or failed), from OpenBoardApp.
- **Officers**, from OpenBoardApp (officers that OpenCharterApp manages show there too).
- **Your apps:** open any app straight into this organization.

You see only what your role in each app lets you see.
