You sign in once at [openapphub.org](https://openapphub.org/login) and stay signed in across the apps.

## Sign in

1. Go to [openapphub.org/login](https://openapphub.org/login).
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**, then select **Sign in**. Or select **Continue with Google**.

## Create an account

1. Select **Create account**.
2. Enter **Your name**, **Email** and a **Password** of at least 8 characters.
3. Agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, then select **Create account**.
4. Open the confirmation link we email you. The [guided setup](/hub/start/getting-started/) then helps you
   name your organization and invite your team.

### No confirmation email

- It can take a minute or two. Check your spam or promotions folder.
- Select **Send it again** on the **Check your email** page, or **Use a different email** if you mistyped it.

If you came from an OpenCharterApp join page, you go back there after creating your account to finish
joining your association.

:::note[Sign-ups are closed for now]
Until OpenAppHub opens to new accounts, the sign-up page shows **Sign-ups are closed for now**.
:::

## Forgot your password

1. On the sign-in page, select **Forgot password?**.
2. Enter your email and select **Send reset link**.
3. Open the link in the email (it works once and expires after an hour), then **Choose a new password**
   and select **Save password**.

Problems? See [Troubleshooting](/hub/help/troubleshooting/).
