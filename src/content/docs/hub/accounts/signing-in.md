Each app has its own sign-in page. The steps are the same in all three.

## Create an account

1. Open the app's sign-in page and choose **Create an account** (or sign up).
2. Enter your name, email and a password, or use **Continue with Google**.
3. If the app asks you to confirm your email, open the link it sends.

By creating an account you agree to the app's terms of service and privacy policy, linked on the sign-in page.

## Sign in

Use your email and password, or **Continue with Google** if that's how you signed up.

If the app says your email or password is incorrect, it doesn't say which one. That's deliberate: it stops strangers from finding out who has an account.

:::tip
Signed up with Google? Use the Google button. Your account has no password, so email-and-password sign-in won't work.
:::

## Forgot your password

1. On the sign-in page, choose **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email and send the reset link.
3. Open the link in the email and choose a new password.

You'll see the same message whether or not the email has an account, for the same privacy reason as above. If no email arrives within a few minutes, check your spam folder, then check you used the address you signed up with.

## Two-step sign-in

OpenLedgerApp supports two-step sign-in with an authenticator app, and an organization can require it. See [Security in OpenLedgerApp](/ledger/admin/security/).

## Change your name, email or password

- **OpenCharterApp:** sidebar → **Account**. See [Your account](/charter/start/your-account/).
- **OpenLedgerApp:** **Settings → Security** for your password, two-step sign-in and deleting your account.
- **OpenBoardApp:** your name and email come from your account. Ask your organization's owner or administrator if your details on the board roster are wrong.
