People join an organization by invitation. Each invitation is for one email address, works **once**, and
expires after **14 days**.

## Invite someone

Owners and admins can invite people:

1. Open **Members**.
2. Under **Invite someone**, enter their **Email** and choose:
   - their **Role in OpenAppHub** (Admin, Member or Viewer);
   - a **Named role**, if your organization has any;
   - their **Role in each app**. Each says what the named role gives (for example *From Treasurer:
     Admin*), or **Not set: viewer**. Anything you leave unset is view-only, and you can change it later.
3. Select **Send invitation**.

The roles in OpenAppHub and in each app are separate ([Roles](/hub/accounts/organizations-and-roles/#roles)).
An invitation sent from inside OpenBoardApp or OpenLedgerApp makes the person an OpenAppHub member with the
role you chose in that app.

- **New to OpenAppHub:** they get an email with a link to join.
- **Already have an account:** we don't email them. You get the link to send them yourself, with a
  **Copy** button.

Pending invitations are listed on the Members page, where you can **Cancel** one. The link then stops
working.

## Accept an invitation

1. Open the link in the invitation email.
2. The page shows the organization and the role you're invited to. If you're not signed in, select
   **Sign in to accept** and sign in or create an account with the email address the invitation was sent to.
3. Select **Join**. If you were new to OpenAppHub, choose a password next.

If the invitation is for one app (for example OpenCharterApp), the link takes you to that app to finish.

## If the link doesn't work

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| **This invitation link isn't valid** | Check you copied the whole link, or ask for a new invitation. |
| **This invitation has expired** | Ask the person who invited you to send a new one. |
| **This invitation was cancelled** | Ask for a new invitation. |
| **This invitation has already been used** | Each invitation works once. If it was yours, just sign in. |
