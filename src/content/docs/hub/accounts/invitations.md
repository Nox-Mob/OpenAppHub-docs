People join an organization by invitation. Each invitation is for one email address, works **once**, and
expires after **14 days**.

## Accept an invitation

1. Open the link in the invitation email.
2. The page shows the organization and the role you're invited to. If you're not signed in, select
   **Sign in to accept** and sign in or create an account with the email address the invitation was sent to.
3. Select **Join**.

If the invitation is for one app (for example OpenCharterApp), the link takes you to that app to finish.

## If the link doesn't work

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| **This invitation link isn't valid** | Check you copied the whole link, or ask for a new invitation. |
| **This invitation has expired** | Ask the person who invited you to send a new one. |
| **This invitation was cancelled** | Ask for a new invitation. |
| **This invitation has already been used** | Each invitation works once. If it was yours, just sign in. |
