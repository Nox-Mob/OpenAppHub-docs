None of the apps send email invitations yet. Instead, an administrator creates an **invitation link** and sends it to the person however they like: email, text or chat.

## How invitation links work

- Each link is for **one person** and works **once**.
- Links **expire**: after 14 days in OpenCharterApp and OpenBoardApp, and after 7 days in OpenLedgerApp.
- Opening a link doesn't accept it. The person signs in (or creates an account) and then presses the button to accept.
- An administrator can **revoke** a link that hasn't been used.
- The link carries the role the administrator chose, such as Treasurer or Board member.

## Accepting an invitation

1. Open the link.
2. Sign in, or create an account if you don't have one.
3. Check the organization name and press the button to accept.

You're now a member of that organization, and it appears in your organization switcher.

## When a link doesn't work

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| The link says it's used | Someone already accepted it, possibly you | Sign in and check your organization list. If it's not there, ask for a new link. |
| The link says it's expired or unavailable | It ran out of time or was revoked | Ask the administrator for a new link. |
| Accepting is refused | You may be signed in to a different account | Sign out, then sign in with the account the invitation is meant for. |

Invitation links are secrets. Anyone with an unused link can accept it, so send it only to the person it's for.
