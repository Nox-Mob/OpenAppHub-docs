A national (or any organization with chapters) runs its chapters in OpenCharterApp. Most chapters need nothing more. When a chapter wants to run **its own board** in OpenBoardApp (meetings, minutes, votes), it gets **its own organization** in OpenAppHub, under its national.

## How a chapter gets one

The national chooses, in OpenCharterApp under **Board settings** > **Chapters on OpenBoardApp**:

| Choice                                          | What happens                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Chapters ask, and you approve** (the default) | A chapter admin selects **Ask to use OpenBoardApp** on the chapter's **Board** tab. The national's owners and admins get an email and approve or decline it on the same **Board settings** page (a declined chapter sees the reason, and can ask again) |
| **Any chapter can turn it on**                  | A chapter admin selects **Use OpenBoardApp** and it's ready straight away                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Chapters can't use OpenBoardApp**             | Chapters don't see the option                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |

Once it's approved (or turned on), OpenAppHub creates the chapter's organization automatically. The chapter admin who asked becomes its **owner**, and the national's owners become its **admins**, so the national can always help.

The chapter's organization shows its national's name in the organization switcher, so it's clear whose chapter it is.

## Billing

A chapter's organization **doesn't have a plan of its own**: it's covered by its national's plan. The chapter's **Billing** page says so and names the national. If the national's plan lapses, the chapter's apps become read-only too.

## Limits

- One level only: a chapter's organization can't have chapters of its own.
- A chapter's organization is made only through OpenCharterApp's approval, not with **Create organization**.
- Officers chosen in OpenCharterApp show up as the chapter's board members in OpenBoardApp ([board members in both apps](/charter/governance/board-seats/)).
