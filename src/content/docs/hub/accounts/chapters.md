Some organizations have chapters. A state association might have one chapter in each city, for example. The national (the main organization) runs all of its chapters in OpenCharterApp. For most chapters, that's all they need.

Sometimes a chapter wants to run **its own board** in OpenBoardApp, with its own meetings, minutes and votes. To do that, the chapter needs **its own organization** in OpenAppHub. This page explains how a chapter gets one and how it's paid for.

## How a chapter gets its own organization

The national decides how this works. The setting is in OpenCharterApp, under **Board settings** > **Chapters on OpenBoardApp**. There are three choices.

**Chapters ask, and you approve.** This is the default.

1. A chapter admin opens the chapter's **Board** tab and selects **Ask to use OpenBoardApp**.
2. The national's owners and admins get an email about the request.
3. They approve or decline it on the same **Board settings** page.
4. If they decline, the chapter sees the reason. The chapter can fix the problem and ask again.

**Any chapter can turn it on.** A chapter admin selects **Use OpenBoardApp**, and it's ready right away. Nobody needs to approve it.

**Chapters can't use OpenBoardApp.** Chapters don't see the option at all.

### What happens next

When a request is approved (or a chapter turns it on), OpenAppHub creates the chapter's organization for you. Nobody has to set it up by hand.

- The chapter admin who asked becomes the **owner** of the new organization.
- The national's owners become **admins** of it. That way the national can always step in and help.

The chapter's organization shows its national's name in the organization switcher. That makes it clear which national it belongs to. For example, you might see "Northside Chapter" with "Riverside Association" under it.

## Billing

A chapter's organization **doesn't need its own plan**. It's covered by its national's plan.

- The chapter's **Billing** page says this and names the national.
- If the national's plan ends or a payment fails for too long, the chapter's apps become read-only too. People can still see everything, but they can't change anything until the national's plan is active again.

## Limits

- **Only one level.** A chapter's organization can't have chapters of its own.
- **Made only through OpenCharterApp.** You can't make a chapter's organization with **Create organization**. It only comes from the approval in OpenCharterApp.
- **Officers carry over.** The officers chosen in OpenCharterApp, like the chapter's president and treasurer, show up as the chapter's board members in OpenBoardApp. See [board members in both apps](/charter/governance/board-seats/).
