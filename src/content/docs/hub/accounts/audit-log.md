---
title: "Audit log"
description: "A record of changes to your organization, its members and invitations."
sidebar:
  order: 6
---

Owners and admins can open **Audit log** in the side menu. It lists, newest first, who changed what:

- the organization being created or renamed, and changes to its time zone or currency;
- people joining, leaving, being removed or changing roles, and ownership transfers;
- invitations being sent, accepted or cancelled.

Times are shown in your organization's time zone. Select **Show older** for earlier entries.

The log can't be edited, and it's kept even after an organization is deleted. Each app keeps its own record
of changes to its data; see that app's docs.
