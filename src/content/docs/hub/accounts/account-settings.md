Select your initials at the top right, then **Account**.

## Name, email and password

- **Your name:** shown to the people in your organizations.
- **Email address:** enter the new address and select **Change email**. Open the confirmation link we send
  to the new address; your email changes once you do.
- **Password:** enter your current password, then the new one twice (at least 8 characters). If you sign in
  with Google, you can **Set password** to also sign in with your email.

## Email notifications

| Notification | Default | Can turn off |
|---|---|---|
| **Account, security and legal notices**: sign-in and password changes, invitations, changes to the Terms or Privacy Policy | On | No |
| **Billing**: trial reminders, receipts and payment problems, for organizations you own or administer | On | Yes |
| **Organization activity**: people joining, leaving or changing roles | On | Yes |
| **Product news**: new features, a few times a year | Off | Yes |

When the Terms of Service or Privacy Policy change, we email everyone with an account before the change takes
effect. Continuing to use OpenAppHub after that means you accept the new version.

## Signed-in devices

**Sign out everywhere** signs you out of OpenAppHub and every app, on every device, including the one you're
using.

## Appearance and cookies

Choose **Light**, **Dark** or **System**, and change your cookie choices. See
[Using the site](/hub/start/using-the-site/).

## Delete your account

Select **Delete my account**. This deletes your sign-in and removes you from every organization. It can't be
undone.

You can't delete your account while you own an organization: first
[transfer ownership](/hub/accounts/organizations-and-roles/#transferring-ownership) or delete the
organization.
