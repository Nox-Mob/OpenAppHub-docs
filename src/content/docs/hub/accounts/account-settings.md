Select your initials at the top right, then **Account**.

## Name, email and password

- **Your name:** shown to the people in your organizations.
- **Email address:** enter the new address and select **Change email**. Open the confirmation link we send
  to the new address; your email changes once you do.
- **Password:** enter your current password, then the new one twice (at least 8 characters). If you sign in
  with Google, you can **Set password** to also sign in with your email.

## Notifications

What you're told about, under the bell and by email, each chosen on its own: see [Notifications](/hub/accounts/notifications/).

## Your organizations

If you belong to more than one organization, choose the one that **opens first** when you sign in. Switching organizations at the top of any page still works as before; this is just where you start. **No default** goes back to the first by name.

## Emails

| Kind of email                                                                                                                                            | Default                | Can turn off                                                                                       |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Account, security and legal notices**: sign-in and password changes, invitations, changes to the Terms or Privacy Policy                               | On                     | No                                                                                                 |
| **Billing**: trial reminders, receipts and payment problems, for organizations you own or administer ([notifications](/hub/accounts/notifications/))     | On                     | Yes                                                                                                |
| **Organization activity**: people joining, leaving or changing roles, and workflows that couldn't finish ([notifications](/hub/accounts/notifications/)) | On                     | Yes                                                                                                |
| **Product news**: new features, a few times a year                                                                                                       | Off                    | Yes                                                                                                |
| **Reminders**: upcoming board meetings and action items due, from the apps you use                                                                       | On                     | Yes                                                                                                |
| **Task summary**: your open tasks and upcoming meetings from every app, in one email                                                                     | Weekly, Monday 7:00 AM | Daily, weekly or off; you also choose the day, time, time zone, organizations and what it includes |

Choose and select **Save**. Turning a kind off here stops those emails, whatever you choose for each notification. These choices are yours alone (nobody else in your organizations sees them) and apply in every app. The task summary has its own page: [The task summary email](/hub/accounts/task-summary/).

Reminders and the task summary are different things:

- **Reminders** are about one thing each, sent when it's coming up (a meeting in 7 days, an action item due in 2). Your organization's owners and admins choose _when_ they're sent ([Automations](/hub/automations/)); you choose _whether_ you get them here.
- **The task summary** is one email that lists everything open for you, on your own schedule.

Every email you can turn off has an **Unsubscribe** link at the bottom; it asks you to confirm first.

When the Terms of Service or Privacy Policy change, we email everyone with an account before the change takes
effect. Continuing to use OpenAppHub after that means you accept the new version.

## Signed-in devices

**Sign out everywhere** signs you out of OpenAppHub and every app, on every device, including the one you're
using.

## Appearance and cookies

Choose **Light**, **Dark** or **System**, and change your cookie choices. See
[Using the site](/hub/start/using-the-site/).

## Delete your account

Select **Delete my account**. This deletes your sign-in and removes you from every organization. It can't be
undone.

You can't delete your account while you own an organization: first
[transfer ownership](/hub/accounts/organizations-and-roles/#transferring-ownership) or delete the
organization.
