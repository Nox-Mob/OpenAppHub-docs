The task summary (sometimes called a digest) is one email that lists what's waiting for you in every app you use: the same tasks you see on your [dashboard](/hub/start/dashboard/), plus the board meetings coming up. It's sent on the schedule you choose, and **only when there's something to tell you**: if nothing is open, no email is sent.

## Choose when you get it

Select your initials at the top right, then **Account**, and find **Task summary** under **Emails**. On a self-hosted app, it's on the app's **Email** page.

| Setting           | What it does                                                                                       | Default                             |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| **How often**     | **Weekly**, **Daily** or **Off**                                                                   | Weekly                              |
| **Day**           | Weekly only: the day of the week it arrives                                                        | Monday                              |
| **Time**          | The hour it's sent, on the hour. It arrives within about 15 minutes of that time                   | 7:00 AM                             |
| **Time zone**     | The time zone for the day and time above                                                           | Your first organization's time zone |
| **Include**       | **Open tasks**, **Upcoming board meetings**, or both. Choose at least one                          | Both                                |
| **Organizations** | Shown when you belong to more than one. Clear an organization to leave it out. Choose at least one | All of them                         |

Under the settings, a line says what you chose in plain words, for example _"Every Monday at about 7:00 AM (America/Chicago)."_ Select **Save** when you're done.

You get at most one summary a day. If you change your settings after today's summary went out, the new settings apply from the next one.

## What's in it

The email has one section for each organization with something to report (if you chose only one organization, or only one has something, it's a single list). Each item links straight to the page where you deal with it, in the right organization.

| From               | What you see                                                                                               | Who sees it                                                                                         |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **OpenBoardApp**   | Action items assigned to you that are open or in progress, with their due date                             | The person they're assigned to                                                                      |
| **OpenCharterApp** | Join requests to review, memberships about to expire, board seats to fill and other tasks                  | Owners and admins of the organization, and people who manage that chapter or unit in OpenCharterApp |
| **OpenLedgerApp**  | Recurring transactions that are due to be reviewed and posted                                              | Owners, admins and treasurers                                                                       |
| **Meetings**       | Scheduled board meetings in the next 7 days (daily summary: the next 2 days), with the date and start time | Everyone in the organization                                                                        |

Items past their due date are marked **overdue** and shown in red.

The subject line says how many tasks are open, for example _"5 open tasks this week: Riverside Community Association"_, or _"Coming up this week: …"_ when there are only meetings.

## Stop getting it

- Set **How often** to **Off** and select **Save**, or
- Select **Unsubscribe** at the bottom of any summary email, then confirm.

Turning the summary off doesn't change your **Reminders**; those are a separate choice on the same page.

## Questions

**I didn't get a summary.** Either nothing was open for you that day (no email is sent then), the summary is off, or it isn't your chosen day and time yet. Check **Account** > **Task summary**. Also check your spam folder, and that your email address is right.

**A task in my summary is already done.** The summary shows what was open when it was sent. Open the item to see where it stands now.

**Why don't I see another person's tasks?** You see the tasks that are yours to do. Owners and admins see their organization's tasks; everyone else sees what's assigned to them.
