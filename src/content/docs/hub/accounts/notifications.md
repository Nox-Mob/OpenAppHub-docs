Notifications tell you when something happens that matters to you: a payment didn't go through, someone joined your organization, a workflow gave you a task. They're the same in every app, and **you choose what you get**.

Notifications are part of OpenAppHub itself. They aren't [workflows](/hub/automations/): your organization doesn't build them, and nobody else chooses yours.

## The bell

The **bell** at the top of every app shows how many notifications you haven't read. Select it to see the latest; select one to open what it's about (in whichever app that is). **Mark all read** clears the count.

You see your own notifications only, from every organization you belong to. They're kept for a year.

## What you're told about

| Notification | Who gets it, unless they turn it off | Who else can turn it on |
|---|---|---|
| **Your trial is ending**: three days before the free trial ends, and again the day before | The owner, and the billing contact by email | Admins |
| **A payment didn't go through** | The owner, and the billing contact by email | Admins |
| **The plan changed**: a new plan or apps, the plan set to end, or ended | The owner, and the billing contact by email | Admins |
| **Someone joined** the organization | The owner | Admins |
| **Someone left** the organization | The owner | Admins |
| **Someone's role changed**: in OpenAppHub, their named role, or their role in an app | The owner | Admins |
| **A task is waiting for you**: a workflow gave you a task (the email says what it is, and may have a **Mark it done** button) | You | — |
| **Something is waiting for your approval**: a workflow asks you to approve or decline something, such as *An award submission is pending your approval* ([Approvals](/hub/automations/approvals/)) | The people asked | — |
| **Someone asked to join** (OpenCharterApp): *Pat Lee has asked to join Riverside. This is pending your approval* ([Reviewing join requests](/charter/membership/join-requests/)) | The people who review applications there | — |
| **A workflow couldn't finish** | The owner | Admins |

You aren't told about something you did yourself: an owner who adds someone isn't notified that they joined.

Notifications about something **waiting for you** (a task, an approval, a join request) go only to the people concerned: nobody else can turn them on. Their emails say what's waiting, link to where it's done, and, if your organization allows it, have buttons to do it straight from the email ([Actions from email](/hub/automations/actions-from-email/)).

The **billing contact** is the address set under **Billing contact** in [organization settings](/hub/accounts/organizations-and-roles/#organization-settings). It gets billing notifications by email even if it isn't anyone's account.

## Choose what you get

Select your initials at the top right, then **Account**, and find **Notifications**. On a self-hosted app, it's on the app's **Email** page. For each notification:

- **In the app**: it shows under the bell.
- **Email**: we email it to you.

Tick either, both or neither; it's saved straight away. **Use the defaults** puts one back the way it was. A notification you don't get unless you turn it on (an admin's billing notifications, for example) says so.

The **Emails** section below still works as a whole: turning off **Billing** or **Organization activity** emails there stops those emails whatever you choose for each notification. Emails about your account, sign-in and the Terms are always sent.
