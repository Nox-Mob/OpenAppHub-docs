Everything in OpenAppHub belongs to an **organization**: your association, board or nonprofit. You can
belong to more than one; the organization switcher at the top of each app shows which one you're working in,
and your choice carries across the apps.

## Roles

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything, including deleting the organization and transferring ownership. Exactly one per organization. |
| **Admin** | Manage people, invitations and settings. |
| **Member** | Use the apps and make changes. |
| **Viewer** | See, but not change. |

Apps can add their own roles on top (for example a board's chair or secretary, or a treasurer). Those never
give more access than the person's role here.

## Ownership

An organization always has exactly one owner. The owner can't leave or be removed until they transfer
ownership to another member, who must already belong to the organization.
