## Organizations

Everything you record belongs to an **organization**: your association, board or business. People in one organization can never see another organization's records, even on the same hosted service. Each app enforces this in its database, not only on the screen.

You can belong to more than one organization. Use the **organization switcher** in the sidebar to move between them.

## Roles

Each app has roles that decide what people can see and do.

| App | Roles | Details |
|---|---|---|
| OpenCharterApp | Built-in and custom roles, with permissions you choose. Permissions flow down from a national to its chapters, never up. | [Roles and permissions](/charter/setup/roles/) |
| OpenBoardApp | Owner, Administrator, Chair, Secretary, Treasurer, Board member, Observer | [Roles and permissions](/board/people/roles/) |
| OpenLedgerApp | Admin, Treasurer, Member, View only | [Members and roles](/ledger/admin/members-and-roles/) |

In OpenBoardApp, a person's **board title** (for example "Vice President") is separate from their **app role**. The title records who holds the office; the role decides what they can do in the app.

## Ownership

OpenBoardApp and OpenLedgerApp have one **owner** per organization. Only the owner can transfer ownership or delete the organization. If the owner is leaving, they should transfer ownership first.
