| What you see                                                         | What to do                                                                                                                  |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **That email and password don't match an account.**                  | Check the email, or use **Forgot password?**.                                                                               |
| **Confirm your email address first. Check your inbox for the link.** | Open the confirmation email we sent when you signed up. Check spam.                                                         |
| **An account with this email already exists. Sign in instead.**      | Sign in, or reset your password.                                                                                            |
| **Too many attempts. Wait a minute and try again.**                  | Wait a minute, then try again.                                                                                              |
| **Sign-ups are closed for now**                                      | OpenAppHub isn't open to new accounts yet.                                                                                  |
| **This link has expired** (password reset)                           | Reset links work once and expire after an hour. Send a new one.                                                             |
| **Continue with Google** shows an error                              | Google sign-in may not be switched on yet. Use your email and password.                                                     |
| No confirmation email after signing up                               | Check spam, then select **Send it again** on the **Check your email** page.                                                 |
| **Your current password isn't right.** (changing your password)      | Enter the password you sign in with now. Forgot it? Sign out and use **Forgot password?**.                                  |
| A member is missing from **Transfer ownership**                      | Only people who have confirmed their email address are listed. Ask them to accept their invitation and confirm their email. |
| **Type the new owner's name exactly as shown to confirm.**           | Type the name shown next to "Type … to confirm", or their email address if that's what's shown.                             |
| You can't delete your account                                        | You own an organization. Transfer ownership or delete the organization first.                                               |
| **Only the owner or an admin can change these settings.**            | Ask an owner or admin in your organization.                                                                                 |

## Two-step sign-in problems

**My code doesn't work.** Codes change every 30 seconds. Wait for a new code and type it right away. If codes never work, check that your phone's clock is set automatically (in your phone's date and time settings). Authenticator codes depend on the right time.

**I see "One more step" in every app.** That's normal after you sign in with only your password. Type your code once, and every app works.

**My organization says it requires two-step sign-in.** Your organization requires it for its owner and admins. Set it up on your account page, then sign out and sign in again with your code. See [When your organization requires it](/hub/accounts/two-step-sign-in/#when-your-organization-requires-it).

**I lost my phone.** See [If you lose your phone](/hub/accounts/two-step-sign-in/#if-you-lose-your-phone).

**It says to sign in again before I can delete or hand over the organization.** For actions that can't be undone, you must have signed in within the last 15 minutes. Sign out, sign in again, and try right away.
