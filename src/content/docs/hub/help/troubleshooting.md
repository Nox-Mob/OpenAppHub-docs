description: "Fixes for common sign-in and account problems."
sidebar:
  order: 2
---

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| **That email and password don't match an account.** | Check the email, or use **Forgot password?**. |
| **Confirm your email address first. Check your inbox for the link.** | Open the confirmation email we sent when you signed up. Check spam. |
| **An account with this email already exists. Sign in instead.** | Sign in, or reset your password. |
| **Too many attempts. Wait a minute and try again.** | Wait a minute, then try again. |
| **Sign-ups are closed for now** | OpenAppHub isn't open to new accounts yet. |
| **This link has expired** (password reset) | Reset links work once and expire after an hour. Send a new one. |
| **Continue with Google** shows an error | Google sign-in may not be switched on yet. Use your email and password. |
