description: "Terms used across OpenAppHub."
sidebar:
  order: 5
---

**Organization**: your association, board or nonprofit. Everything in the apps belongs to one.

**Owner**: the one person who can delete the organization or transfer ownership.

**Role**: what someone can do in an organization: owner, admin, member or viewer.

**Invitation**: a single-use link that lets someone join an organization. Expires after 14 days.

**Read-only**: you can see and export everything but not change it, for example after a trial ends.

**Self-hosted**: running an app on your own server instead of openapphub.org.
