Here's what the words in OpenAppHub mean. They're in alphabetical order.

**Action from email**: a button in an email, like **Approve**, **Decline** or **Mark it done**, that does the job for you. You sign in, confirm, and it's done. Your organization decides whether these buttons are allowed. See [Actions from email](/hub/automations/actions-from-email/).

**Approval**: a step in a workflow that asks people to approve or decline something, and then waits for their answer. See [Approvals](/hub/automations/approvals/).

**Audit log**: a list of changes: who changed what, and when. It helps you see what happened and spot mistakes. Also called **History**.

**Authenticator app**: an app on your phone that shows a new 6-digit code every 30 seconds. Examples are Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator and 1Password. You use it for two-step sign-in.

**Automation**: something the apps do on their own for your organization, like sending reminder emails. The owner and admins can turn one off or change when it runs. See [Automations](/hub/automations/).

**Chapter's organization**: the organization a chapter gets in OpenAppHub when it runs its own board in OpenBoardApp. It's paid for by its national's plan. See [Chapters' own organizations](/hub/accounts/chapters/).

**Default organization**: the organization that opens first when you sign in. You choose it on your account page.

**Invitation**: a link that lets one person join your organization. It works once and stops working after 14 days.

**Named role**: a role your organization makes up, like _Treasurer_, that sets a person's role in each app at once. See [Organizations and roles](/hub/accounts/organizations-and-roles/).

**No access**: a role in one app that keeps a person out of that app completely. They stay in your organization and keep their roles in the other apps.

**Notification**: a message OpenAppHub sends you, under the bell at the top of the page and by email. Each person chooses which ones they get. See [Notifications](/hub/accounts/notifications/).

**Organization**: your association, board or nonprofit. Everything in the apps belongs to one organization.

**Owner**: the one person who can delete the organization or hand it over to someone else. Every organization has exactly one owner.

**Read-only**: you can see and export everything, but you can't change anything. This happens, for example, when a free trial ends without a plan.

**Recent sign-in**: some actions are so serious that OpenAppHub asks you to have signed in within the last 15 minutes. These are deleting an organization, handing over ownership and deleting your account. If it's been longer, sign out and sign in again.

**Role**: what someone is allowed to do: owner, admin, member or viewer. Each person has a role in OpenAppHub and a role in each app.

**Run**: one time a workflow goes through its steps. Each run is listed under **What happened**.

**Self-hosted**: running an app on your own server instead of using openapphub.org.

**Step**: one thing a workflow does, like sending an email, giving someone a task, asking for approval or looking something up. See [Steps reference](/hub/automations/steps/).

**Task summary**: one email with your open tasks and upcoming meetings from every app, sent on the schedule you choose. Also called a digest. See [The task summary email](/hub/accounts/task-summary/).

**Template**: a ready-made workflow. You change it to fit your organization and save it as your own. See [Templates](/hub/automations/templates/).

**Trigger**: what starts a workflow, like _A payment is received_ or _On a schedule_. See [What starts a workflow](/hub/automations/triggers/).

**Two-step sign-in**: signing in with your password **and** a 6-digit code from an authenticator app. Even if someone learns your password, they can't get in without your phone. Some people call it two-factor authentication, 2FA or MFA. See [Two-step sign-in](/hub/accounts/two-step-sign-in/).

**Workflow**: an automation your organization builds: _when_ something happens (or on a schedule), _then_ do these steps. See [Automations](/hub/automations/).
