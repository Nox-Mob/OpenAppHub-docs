1. **Search these docs** with the search box at the top of the page.
2. **Check the app's FAQ and troubleshooting pages:** [OpenCharterApp](/charter/help/faq/), [OpenBoardApp](/board/help/faq/), [OpenLedgerApp](/ledger/help/faq/).
3. **Email support:** [support@openapphub.org](mailto:support@openapphub.org).

## Reporting a problem

Include:

- which app, and whether it's the hosted version, self-hosted or the desktop app;
- what you were doing, step by step;
- what you expected and what happened instead, with the exact wording of any message;
- a screenshot if you can;
- your browser, and whether you were on a phone.

Don't send passwords, card numbers or invitation links.

OpenLedgerApp has a **Diagnostics** page (Settings → Diagnostics) that produces a copyable report with no names or amounts. Paste it into your email.

## Suggesting a feature

Email support, or open an issue in the app's GitHub repository:
[OpenCharterApp](https://github.com/Nox-Mob/OpenCharterApp/issues),
[OpenBoardApp](https://github.com/Nox-Mob/OpenBoardApp/issues),
[OpenLedgerApp](https://github.com/Nox-Mob/OpenLedgerApp/issues).
