OpenAppHub is growing. This page lists the things it can't do yet. We'd rather you know now than find out halfway through a job.

## What works today

You can already do all of these:

- Create an account and sign in, with two-step sign-in if you want it.
- Set up an organization and invite people to it.
- Give each person a role in OpenAppHub and in each app.
- Hand over ownership of an organization to someone else.
- Change your own account settings.
- See the audit log, which lists who changed what.
- See your free trial, or choose a plan, on the **Billing** page.

## What doesn't work yet

**Some tasks can't be finished from the dashboard.** You can finish three kinds right on your OpenAppHub dashboard: action items from OpenBoardApp, recurring entries from OpenLedgerApp, and tasks from your workflows. Other tasks open in their own app. That's on purpose. Those tasks are real work, like reviewing a member's application, and the app is the right place to do it.

**Invitations to people who already have an account aren't emailed.** If the person you invite already uses OpenAppHub, copy the invitation link and send it to them yourself. People who are new to OpenAppHub get the email as usual.

**You can't run a workflow by hand.** A workflow runs when its trigger happens, or on its schedule. To see what a workflow would do right now, use **Test run**. A test run shows each step, but it doesn't send emails or change anything.

**Some workflow triggers aren't here yet.** Triggers for forms, award requests and elections will arrive when those features are added to OpenCharterApp.

**Only people in your organization can answer approvals.** An approval step in a workflow can only ask people who have an account in your organization. You can't ask someone outside it, like an outside accountant, to approve something.

Something missing that you need? Email support@openapphub.org and tell us what you're trying to do.
