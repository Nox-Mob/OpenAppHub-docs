title: "Known limits"
description: "What OpenAppHub can't do yet."
sidebar:
  order: 3
---

Today you can create an account, set up an organization, invite people, manage roles, transfer ownership,
change your account settings, see the audit log, and see your trial or choose a plan on **Billing**. Not available yet:

- Only some tasks can be finished from the dashboard (OpenBoardApp action items, OpenLedgerApp recurring entries, workflow tasks); the rest open in their app, because they mean real work there.
- Invitations to people who already have an account aren't emailed; you send them the link yourself.
- Workflows can't be run by hand (use a schedule, or **Test run** to see what one would do).
- Workflow triggers for forms, award requests and elections arrive with those features in OpenCharterApp.
- Approvals are answered by people with an account in your organization only.
