---
title: "Known limits"
description: "What OpenAppHub can't do yet."
sidebar:
  order: 3
---

Today you can create an account, set up an organization, invite people, manage roles, transfer ownership,
change your account settings, see the audit log, and see your trial on **Billing**. Not available yet:

- Prices aren't announced yet, so plans can't be bought: **Billing** shows them as **Price TBA**, and the
  free trial continues.
- One sign-in across the apps: each app moves onto OpenAppHub separately. Until then, an app's dashboard card
  says **Coming to OpenAppHub soon**.
- Invitations to people who already have an account aren't emailed; you send them the link yourself.
- Notification preferences are saved, but billing and organization-activity emails aren't sent yet.
