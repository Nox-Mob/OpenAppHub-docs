## Next up for each app

- **OpenCharterApp:** automatic email (invitations, receipts and renewal reminders), then postpaid dues and invoices, approvals, board reports, documents and elections.
- **OpenBoardApp:** meeting reminders and a tested backup-restore routine.
- **OpenLedgerApp:** the rest of the features in the desktop app (history, exports, backups, bank import), a self-hosting installer, and testing with non-accountants on the way to version 1.0.
- **The suite:** one sign-in and one organization across all apps, and hosted plans.

Plans change. The repositories are the source of truth.
