## Is it really free?

The software is free and open source. Running it yourself costs only your own hosting. The OpenLedgerApp desktop app is free. We'll also offer paid hosting for people who don't want to run servers; see [Hosted or self-hosted](/hub/hosting/hosted-vs-self-hosted/).

## Do I need all three apps?

No. Each app works on its own. See [Which app do I need?](/hub/start/which-app/)

## Why do I have to sign in to each app separately?

Today each app has its own accounts. A single sign-in for the whole suite is planned; see [How the apps fit together](/hub/start/how-the-apps-fit/).

## Can other organizations see our data?

No. Each organization's records are separated by the database itself. See [Privacy and security](/hub/data/privacy-and-security/).

## What happens to our data if we stop using an app?

Export it first; see [Your data and exporting it](/hub/data/ownership-and-export/). Your exports are ordinary CSV, Excel, PDF and ZIP files that open without the app.

## Can I try an app without real data?

Yes. Each app has demo data. OpenBoardApp has a demo organization you can sign in to, OpenLedgerApp has sample organizations for self-hosters, and OpenCharterApp has demo organizations used for testing. Check each app's **Getting started** page.

## Do the apps work on a phone?

Yes, in the phone's web browser. Menus collapse to fit, and wide tables scroll sideways inside their box. There are no phone apps in the app stores.

## Is this accounting, legal or tax advice?

No. The apps help you keep records. Check tax wording on receipts and anything you file with your accountant or lawyer.

## Were the apps built with AI?

Partly. AI coding tools helped write the code, and every app is checked by automated tests of its rules, permissions and security on every change. The code is public, so anyone can review it.
