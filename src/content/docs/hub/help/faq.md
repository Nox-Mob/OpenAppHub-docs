**Do I need a separate account for each app?**
No. One OpenAppHub account works in every app that has moved onto OpenAppHub.

**Do I have to use all three apps?**
No. Use the ones you need.

**What happens when the free trial ends?**
Nothing is deleted. The apps become read-only until you choose a plan, and you can still export everything.

**Is it open source?**
Yes. You can read the code and run each app yourself. See [Hosted or self-hosted](/hub/hosting/hosted-vs-self-hosted/).

**Do dues-paying members of my association need a plan seat?**
No. Members who join and pay dues through OpenCharterApp don't count toward your plan.

**How do I stop getting an email or a notification?**
On your account page, under **Notifications**, untick it (in the app, by email, or both). See [Notifications](/hub/accounts/notifications/). Account and security emails are always sent.

**Can the apps do things on their own, like reminders?**
Yes: ready-made reminders, and workflows your organization builds. See [Automations](/hub/automations/).

**Can I approve things straight from an email?**
If your organization allows it (Organization settings, Actions from email). You still sign in and confirm. See [Actions from email](/hub/automations/actions-from-email/).

**Which organization opens when I sign in?**
The one you last chose, or your default organization (your account page, Your organizations).
