## Accounts and signing in

**Do I need a separate account for each app?**
No. One OpenAppHub account works in every app: OpenCharterApp, OpenBoardApp and OpenLedgerApp. You sign in once.

**What is two-step sign-in, and should I use it?**
Two-step sign-in asks for a 6-digit code from an app on your phone, as well as your password. Even if someone learns your password, they can't get in without your phone. Yes, we recommend it for everyone. See [Two-step sign-in](/hub/accounts/two-step-sign-in/).

**Why does it say "One more step" when I sign in?**
Your account uses two-step sign-in. Open your authenticator app and type the code for OpenAppHub.

**Why can't I open my organization? It says it requires two-step sign-in.**
Your organization requires two-step sign-in for its owner and admins, and you're one of them. Set it up on your account page, then sign out and sign in again with your code. See [When your organization requires it](/hub/accounts/two-step-sign-in/#when-your-organization-requires-it).

**Why does it ask me to sign in again before deleting or handing over an organization?**
Those actions can't be undone. So you must have signed in within the last 15 minutes. That way, someone using a computer you forgot to sign out of can't do them.

**Which organization opens when I sign in?**
The one you used last, or your default organization if you chose one. You choose it on your account page, under **Your organizations**.

## Plans and data

**Do I have to use all three apps?**
No. Use the ones you need.

**What happens when the free trial ends?**
Nothing is deleted. The apps become read-only until you choose a plan. You can still see and export everything.

**Do dues-paying members of my association need a plan seat?**
No. Members who join and pay dues through OpenCharterApp don't count toward your plan.

**Who owns our data?**
Your organization does. Owners and admins can export everything from each app at any time. See [Your data and exports](/hub/data/ownership-and-export/).

**Is it open source?**
Yes. Anyone can read the code, and you can run each app on your own server. See [Hosted or self-hosted](/hub/hosting/hosted-vs-self-hosted/).

## Safety

**How do you keep our information safe?**
Every piece of information belongs to one organization, and the database checks every request. Everything travels encrypted, card numbers never reach our servers, and important changes are recorded. See [Security and privacy](/hub/help/security/).

**I think I found a security problem. What do I do?**
Please email support@openapphub.org with "Security" in the subject, and don't share it with anyone else until we've fixed it. See [Reporting a security problem](/hub/help/security/#reporting-a-security-problem).

## Email and automations

**How do I stop getting an email or a notification?**
On your account page, under **Notifications**, untick it: in the app, by email, or both. See [Notifications](/hub/accounts/notifications/). Account and security emails are always sent.

**Can the apps do things on their own, like reminders?**
Yes. There are ready-made reminders, and workflows your organization can build. See [Automations](/hub/automations/).

**Can I approve things straight from an email?**
If your organization allows it. The setting is **Actions from email** in Organization settings. You still sign in and confirm before anything happens. See [Actions from email](/hub/automations/actions-from-email/).
