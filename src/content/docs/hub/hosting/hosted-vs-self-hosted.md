Every app is open source. You can let us run it for you, or run it yourself.

| | Hosted by OpenAppHub | Self-hosted |
|---|---|---|
| Who runs the servers | We do | You do, on your own server or your own Supabase account |
| Updates and backups | Handled for you | Your job |
| Cost | A subscription per organization (plans coming soon) | Free software; you pay for your own hosting |
| Skills needed | None | Comfortable with a database, environment settings and command-line tools |
| Your data | Yours, exportable at any time | Yours, on your own infrastructure |

## OpenLedgerApp also has a desktop app

OpenLedgerApp comes in a third form: a **desktop app** for Windows, macOS and Linux that keeps your books in a file on your own computer, with no account and no internet needed. It's free and meant for one person keeping one organization's books. It's currently a preview with some features still to come. See [The desktop app](/ledger/start/desktop-app/).

If several people need to work on the same books, use the hosted or self-hosted web version instead.

## How to choose

- **You want it to just work:** hosted.
- **You have someone technical and want full control, or must keep data on your own servers:** self-hosted.
- **You're one person doing the books for one small organization:** the OpenLedgerApp desktop app.

You can move later. Each app lets you export your data; see [Exporting your data](/hub/data/ownership-and-export/).
