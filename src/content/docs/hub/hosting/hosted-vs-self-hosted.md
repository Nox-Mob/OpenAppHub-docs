| | Hosted (openapphub.org) | Self-hosted |
|---|---|---|
| Set-up | Create an account | Install the app on your own server and database |
| Sign-in | One account for every app | Each app has its own sign-in |
| Updates and backups | Done for you | Your job |
| Cost | A plan for your organization (prices coming soon) | Free (you pay for your own hosting) |
| Your data | Exportable at any time | On your own database |

Each app runs on its own when self-hosted. See each app's self-hosting page:
[OpenCharterApp](/charter/help/self-hosting/), [OpenBoardApp](/board/help/self-hosting/),
[OpenLedgerApp](/ledger/help/self-hosting/).

## The free trial and billing (hosted)

Every new organization gets a **30-day free trial of every app**, for up to 10 people. No card needed.
Each person gets one free trial; a second organization you create starts without one.

Owners and admins open **Billing** in the side menu to see:

- the plan or trial, with the days left and the end date;
- how many editors the organization has (everyone except viewers), against the plan's limit;
- **Choose a plan**: monthly or yearly. **Starter** asks which app you want. Payment is handled by Stripe;
- **Manage billing** once you have a plan: change plan or payment method, see invoices, or cancel.
  **Update payment** appears if a payment fails.

If a trial or plan ends, **nothing is deleted**: the apps become read-only, and owners and admins can still
export everything. Choose a plan to keep working.

:::note[Prices]
Plans and prices are on [Plans](/hub/hosting/plans/). A plan shows **Price TBA** only if its price isn't
available yet; it can't be chosen until it is.
:::
