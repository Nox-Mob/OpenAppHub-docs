All plans are priced **per organization**, not per person.

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly | Apps | Members | File storage |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free trial | Free for 30 days, no card | — | All three | 10 | 1 GB |
| Starter | $9 | $90 | One app of your choice | 10 | 1 GB |
| Suite | $19 | $190 | All three | 25 | 5 GB |
| Plus | $49 | $490 | All three | Unlimited | 25 GB, priority support |
| Self-hosted | Free | — | Any | Unlimited | Your own |
| OpenLedgerApp desktop | Free | — | OpenLedgerApp only | One person | Your computer |

## When a trial or subscription ends

Your organization becomes **read-only**. Everyone can still sign in, read everything and export all your data. Nothing is deleted. Subscribe again to make changes.

## Changing or cancelling

Owners and administrators manage the plan from OpenAppHub's billing page: upgrade, downgrade, switch between monthly and yearly, update the card, download invoices or cancel. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the period you've paid for.
