Every organization starts with a **30-day free trial** of every app, with up to 10 editors. No card needed.
After that, choose one plan for the organization, paid monthly or yearly in US dollars.

| Plan | Apps | Editors | Monthly | Yearly |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | 1 app of your choice | up to 5 | $5 | $50 |
| Suite | all three | up to 15 | $10 | $100 |
| Plus | all three | unlimited | $25 | $250 |

**Editors** are everyone who can change your data: the owner, admins and members
([roles](/hub/accounts/organizations-and-roles/)). **Viewers don't count**, and neither do dues-paying members
in OpenCharterApp, so a chapter with hundreds of members can still be on Starter or Suite.

Owners and admins choose and change plans on **Billing**. Payment is handled by Stripe. If a plan lapses,
nothing is deleted: the apps become read-only and you can still export everything.

The prices on the **Billing** page are always the current ones.

## Chapters

A chapter that uses OpenBoardApp through its national (see
[Chapters on OpenBoardApp](/charter/governance/board-seats/#chapters-on-openboardapp)) is covered by the
national's plan. Its **Billing** page says so, and it has no plan or bill of its own.
