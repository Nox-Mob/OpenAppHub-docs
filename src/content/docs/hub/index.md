OpenAppHub brings three apps together under one account:

- **OpenCharterApp**: members, chapters and dues for associations.
- **OpenBoardApp**: meetings, minutes and decisions for boards.
- **OpenLedgerApp**: budgets and books for small nonprofits.

You sign in once, at [openapphub.org](https://openapphub.org), and your organization is shared by every app.
Each app is also open source and can run on its own server.
