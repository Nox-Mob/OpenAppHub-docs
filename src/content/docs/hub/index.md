OpenAppHub is a family of open-source apps for the people who keep small organizations running: membership secretaries, board chairs and secretaries, and treasurers.

| App | What it does | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|
| [OpenCharterApp](/charter/) | Members, dues, receipts, board seats and reports | Associations, from one local group to a national with chapters |
| [OpenBoardApp](/board/) | Meetings, agendas, live votes, minutes, documents and action items | Boards of small nonprofits |
| [OpenLedgerApp](/ledger/) | Double-entry bookkeeping, statement checks, budgets, funds and reports | Very small businesses and nonprofits |

Each app works on its own. You can use one, two or all three.

## Where things stand

All three apps are early releases. They work and are tested, but they're still changing, and some features you'd expect aren't there yet. Each app's **Known limits** page lists what's missing.

| App | Current version |
|---|---|
| OpenCharterApp | 0.1.0, the first release an association can run on |
| OpenBoardApp | 0.1 |
| OpenLedgerApp | 0.1.1, pre-release: try it with sample data before trusting it with real books |
