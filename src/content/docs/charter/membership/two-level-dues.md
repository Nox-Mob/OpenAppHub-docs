In the **separate memberships** model, a national can require everyone who joins a chapter to also be a member of the national. Joining then takes two payments: the national's dues first, then the chapter's. Each organization collects its own payment and issues its own receipt.

## Turn it on (national)

1. **Settings → Setup → Membership setup**, step **Rules for your chapters**.
2. Turn on **Must chapter members also be members of your organization?**
3. Optionally set a **chapter price range** (for example lowest $10, highest $30).
4. Apply.

Chapters see the range on their Membership levels page. A chapter price outside the range is refused.

## What a new member sees

1. The chapter's join page mentions **two payments**.
2. Someone who isn't a national member sees **Step 1 of 2: *National* dues** and a **Join … first** button.
3. After paying the national dues, the return page offers **Continue to step 2: chapter dues**.
4. **Step 2 of 2:** they pay the chapter's dues.

Someone who's already a current national member goes straight to the chapter's dues.

## Approvals

If the national approves new members, the chapter application waits. A chapter administrator can't approve it until the national membership is active; the app says so. Approve at the national first, then at the chapter.

## National standing at the chapter

Chapter administrators see a column on the Members page named after the national, showing each member's **national standing** next to their chapter standing. Members whose national membership is missing or lapsed see "Join or renew *National*" in their Actions needed.

## No chapter dues

If chapters may not create their own levels, a chapter's join page sends people to the national to join.
