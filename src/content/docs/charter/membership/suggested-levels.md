In the **separate memberships** model, a national can offer its levels to chapters as a starting point.

## Suggest a level (national)

**Settings → Membership levels**, then on a level press **Suggest to chapters**. Press **Stop suggesting** to withdraw it; chapters that already used it keep their level.

## Use a suggested level (chapter)

**Settings → Membership levels.** The national's level shows **Suggested for you**. Press **Start from this level** and choose:

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Copy it** | Your own level, starting from the national's. Change it freely. Later national changes don't affect it. |
| **Link to it** | A level that follows the national's name, terms and retirement. You choose your price relative to the national's. |

### Linked prices

When linking, choose how your price relates to the national's:

- the **same** price,
- an **amount less**, or
- a **percentage less**.

**Your prices** previews each term. Prices stay within the national's chapter price range. Then press **Link this level**.

A linked level says "Linked to *National*'s *level*: 20% less than …". It has **Price** and **Make it our own**, but no Edit.

- When the national changes its price, yours follows.
- When the national retires the level, yours is retired too.

## Make it our own

**Make it our own** turns a linked level into a copy you can edit freely. It asks you to confirm, because it **can't be undone**: the level stops following the national.

## When chapters can't use suggested levels

If the national doesn't allow chapters to create their own levels, chapters don't see **Start from this level**. In the home-chapter model, chapters have no levels at all.
