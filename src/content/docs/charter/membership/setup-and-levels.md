## Membership setup (recommended)

**Settings → Setup → Membership setup** walks you through everything in order: levels and terms, prices by member fields, joining and renewals, approval, the grace period and (at a national) rules for chapters.

- **Review and apply** shows exactly what will change before anything is saved.
- Your answers are kept as a **draft**, so the browser's Back button or a reload doesn't lose them.
- You can run setup again at any time. It's filled in with your current settings, and the review lists only what you changed.

## Membership levels

A **level** is a kind of membership (for example Regular, Senior, Lifetime). Each level has one or more **terms**.

**Settings → Membership levels → Add level.**

- Give it a name. An organization with only one level can leave it unnamed; it shows as "Membership".
- Add **terms**: a length (days, months or years) and a price in dollars. A term with no length is **lifetime**.

At a national, the page opens with **How membership works here**: the model, approval, grace period and rules for chapters, with links to change them.

## Terms and dates

- A new membership runs from its start date for one term. A one-year membership starting January 1 is **paid through** December 31.
- **Renewing early stacks**: the new term starts the day after the current paid-through date, not today. Members never lose days by renewing early.
- After the paid-through date, the **grace period** keeps the member in good standing for the number of days you set.

See [Member standing](/charter/members/standing/).

## Retiring a level

Levels are retired rather than deleted, so existing memberships keep their meaning. A retired level is no longer offered on the join page.
