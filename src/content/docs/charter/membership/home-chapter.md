In this model, members join the **national**. Each member picks a **home chapter** where they belong locally. The home chapter is a choice, not a second membership.

## Setting it up (national)

1. Choose **National membership with a home chapter** when creating the national, or later on **Settings → General**.
2. Optionally pick an **at-large chapter** on Settings → General, for members without a local chapter (for example an "Online chapter").
3. Set up levels and prices at the national only. Chapters have no Membership levels page in this model.

## Joining

- The national's join page has **Your chapter**, with the at-large chapter first and **None for now** last.
- A chapter's join page sends people to the national's join page with that chapter already selected.

After joining, the member's Overview shows **Your chapter: …**, and the chapter's Members page lists them with their national level and standing. Their Details say "This is the member's national membership".

## Changing chapter

Members change their home chapter from the national's **Overview**. The old chapter stops listing them and the new one starts. History is kept. If they held a seat in the old chapter, the term ends with the reason "changed chapter".

## What's different for administrators

- Settings that don't apply are hidden: chapter levels, chapter dues, chapter approval and the chapter grace period.
- **Membership setup** at the national asks only about chapter logos under "Rules for your chapters". At a chapter, it has no levels or joining steps.
- **Import members** at the national, with a **Chapter** column. Chapters have no Import button.
- To take someone off a chapter, move them or remove them at the national. A chapter record can't be removed on its own.
- **Reports:** the national's total counts each member once, even though they also appear in their chapter's row.

## Not available yet

Local chapter dues on top of national dues (usually billed later rather than paid up front) are planned for a later release.
