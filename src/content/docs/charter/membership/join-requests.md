With **New members need admin approval** on (in **Settings**), someone who joins through your [join page](/charter/setup/chapters-and-join-page/#your-join-page) doesn't become a member straight away: their **application** (join request) waits for someone to review it.

## Who reviews

Anyone who can manage members in that chapter or unit: owners and admins, and anyone whose role there includes managing members (for example a chapter's own admin). A national's admins can review requests for every chapter.

## Being told

Each reviewer is told as soon as someone applies, saying who and where: **Pat Lee has asked to join Riverside. This is pending your approval.**

- **Under the bell**, in every app.
- **By email**, unless they turned that off ([Notifications](/hub/accounts/notifications/)).
- On their OpenAppHub **dashboard**, under **Your tasks** (_Review join request: Pat Lee_), and in their [task summary email](/hub/accounts/task-summary/).

The person who applied isn't one of the people told, even if they're also a reviewer.

## Approving or declining

**In the app:** open the chapter's **Members** tab. Pending applications are marked; open one and choose **Approve** or **Decline**, with an optional note.

**From the email:** if your organization allows **Approve or decline join requests from email** (OpenAppHub **Organization settings**, **Actions from email**; off until an owner or admin turns it on), the email has **Approve** and **Decline** buttons. A button opens a page that asks you to sign in (as yourself, the person it was sent to), says what will happen, and lets you add a note; nothing happens until you **confirm**. Your usual permissions apply: if you can't approve members there any more, it says so. Each button works once, for 7 days. See [Actions from email](/hub/automations/actions-from-email/).

## What happens next

- **Approved:** the membership becomes active; the member's term starts as set by the level.
- **Declined:** the application is closed. If they paid online, refunds are handled as described in [Payments](/charter/money/payments/).
- Either way, it leaves everyone's task list.

Only pending applications can be approved or declined: once someone has decided, the other reviewers' buttons say it was already decided.

## Automating more

Workflows can do more when someone joins: a welcome email, a task to call them. See the _Welcome new members_ template ([Templates](/hub/automations/templates/)).
