## How membership works

A national chooses how people belong to it and its chapters, **once** (when it's set up or adds its first chapter; it can't be changed afterwards):

- **Separate memberships**: the national and each chapter have their own members, levels and dues. A person can belong to several.
- **National membership with a home chapter**: one membership at the national; each member picks a home chapter (or an at-large chapter) and can change it. Chapters don't sell memberships of their own.

A standalone association simply has its own members, levels and dues. More: [Chapters, regions and your join page](/charter/setup/chapters-and-join-page/).

## Levels and terms

A **level** is a kind of membership, like _Regular_, _Family_ or _Life_. Each level has one or more **terms**: how long a membership lasts and what it costs. Find them under **Settings** > **Membership levels**.

| Field                | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Level name**       | What members see, for example _Regular member_                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Description**      | A sentence or two on the join page about who it's for                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Tax treatment**    | What receipts for this level say about taxes: **Tax-deductible**, **Partly tax-deductible** or **Not tax-deductible**. Choose tax-deductible only if your organization can accept deductible gifts (for example a 501(c)(3)) and members get nothing of value in return; ask your accountant if you're not sure. See [receipts](/charter/money/payments/#receipts) |
| **Terms and prices** | One line per term: a length (a number of months or years, or **Lifetime**, which never expires), a label (_1 year_) and a price. Add as many as you offer, for example _1 year $40_ and _3 years $100_                                                                                                                                                             |

You can retire a level or term you no longer offer; existing members keep theirs until it ends.

## Different prices for different members

A term can have **pricing rules**: a different price for members who answer a [member field](/charter/members/roster/) a certain way, for example a lower price for students or veterans, or by rank. Open a term's **Pricing rules**:

- The **usual price** is the term's price. Each rule says **if** (the member's answers) **then** (a price, or a percentage off).
- **The first rule that fits a member, from the top, sets what they pay.** Members who fit no rule pay the usual price.
- **Order** decides which rules are checked first: lower numbers first.
- Members answer these fields on the join page, so the right price shows before they pay.

## Joining and approval

People join on your [join page](/charter/setup/chapters-and-join-page/#your-join-page). With **New members need admin approval** on (in **Settings**), a join request waits for review: the reviewers are told under the bell and by email, it's on their dashboard's tasks and on **Members**, where they approve or decline (or, if your organization allows it, straight from the email). See [Reviewing join requests](/charter/membership/join-requests/). Otherwise people become members as soon as they pay.

You can also add members yourself (**Add member**, or import a spreadsheet: [Members](/charter/members/roster/)) and [record payments by hand](/charter/money/payments/#recording-a-payment-by-hand).

## Renewals, expiry and the grace period

- A membership runs for its term from the day it starts. Renewing while it is current (or in its grace period) extends it from its current end date, so no time is lost; renewing after it has lapsed starts a new term on the day of payment.
- Members renew on the join page; admins can record a renewal by hand.
- When a membership expires, the member keeps their standing for the **Grace period** (30 days unless you change it in **Settings**). After that, their membership has lapsed.
- Memberships expiring in the next 30 days show as a task on your dashboard, so you can follow up.
- Lifetime memberships never expire.

## Chapters' dues

Under **separate memberships**, the national decides (in **Settings**) whether chapters sell their own memberships, whether they may create their own levels, the lowest and highest dues they may charge, and whether chapter members also need a national membership. See the [Settings reference](/charter/setup/settings/#membership).
