---
title: "Membership and dues"
description: "How membership works and how dues are paid."
sidebar:
  order: 1
---

Each national chooses how membership works, once (and can change it later in **Settings**):

- **Separate memberships**: the national and each chapter have their own members, levels and dues.
- **National membership with a home chapter**: one membership at the national; each member picks a home
  chapter (or an at-large chapter) and can change it.

Members join and renew online. Dues go to the organization's **own Stripe account**, and each payment gets a
numbered receipt. Payments made another way can be recorded by hand.
