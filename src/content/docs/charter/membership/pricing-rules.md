A **pricing rule** changes a term's price when a member's fields match a condition. For example: "When Rank is at least E-5, they pay $30", or "When Veteran is yes, 20% off".

## Add a rule

You can add rules in **Membership setup** (the prices step) or directly:

1. **Settings → Membership levels**, then click a term (for example "1 year · $50.00").
2. Add a rule: one or more **conditions** on member fields, and the effect: **they pay** a fixed amount, or a **discount** (amount or percentage).
3. Save. The term now shows "· 2 pricing rules" (or however many), and the list reads in plain words.

## How rules are applied

- Rules are checked **from the top**. The **first** matching rule sets the price; later rules are ignored.
- Use **Order** to move rules up or down.
- A rule with **no conditions** applies to everyone.
- If no rule matches, the member pays the term's normal price.

## What members see

- On the join page, a term with rules says its price can depend on your answers.
- As the member answers the "About you" questions, the pay button shows their price.
- When renewing, the price uses the member's stored fields.
- The payment and the receipt show the price the rule set.

## Retiring a rule

Retire a rule to stop it applying. It greys out but stays in the list for your records.

## Chapter limits

If the national sets a chapter price range, a chapter's rule can't produce a price outside it.
