Every **national** chooses one of two models for how membership works across its chapters. A standalone organization doesn't choose.

:::danger[This choice can't be changed]
Once you pick a model, it's fixed. If you're unsure, choose **Decide later** when creating the national; Settings → General will keep offering the choice until you make it.
:::

## The two models

| | Separate memberships | National membership with a home chapter |
|---|---|---|
| **Who members join** | The national and each chapter have their own members | Members join the national only |
| **Levels and dues** | National and chapters each have levels and dues; optionally two payments (national, then chapter) | Only the national has levels and dues |
| **Chapters** | Each chapter has its own roster | Each member picks a **home chapter**, which can be changed at any time |
| **Members without a local chapter** | Join the national only, if allowed | Pick an **at-large chapter** (for example an online chapter) or "None for now" |
| **A good fit if** | Chapters run their own memberships and money | Membership is really national, and chapters are where members meet |

## How to choose

- Do your chapters set and collect their own dues today? → **Separate memberships**.
- Does a member's card say the national's name, with a local chapter as an affiliation? → **Home chapter**.
- Do you need chapter dues on top of national dues, collected by the chapter? Today that works in the separate model ([two-level dues](/charter/membership/two-level-dues/)). Local chapter dues in the home-chapter model are planned.

## Where to choose

- When creating the national: step 2, **How membership works**. **Create** is refused until you pick one or **Decide later**.
- Later: **Settings → General**, which shows the same comparison and asks you to confirm.

After choosing, Settings → General says the model is chosen and can't be changed.
