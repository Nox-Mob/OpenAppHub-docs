Every payment, online or recorded by hand, gets a **numbered receipt** from the organization that received the money.

## What's on a receipt

- a receipt number in the form **2026-00001** (year, then a running number)
- your legal name, EIN and logo (from Settings → General)
- the payer, items, amount and payment method
- tax wording and your receipt footer

:::caution
The receipt tax wording is a placeholder until it has been reviewed by a CPA. Check it with your accountant before relying on it for tax-deductibility.
:::

## Finding receipts

- **Administrators:** Settings → Payments → the **Receipt** column, or a member's **History**.
- **Members:** Overview → **Your membership → Your receipts**.

## Print

Open a receipt and press **Print**. It fits on one page, and the buttons don't print. To email a receipt, print it to PDF and attach it. (Automatic receipt emails are planned.)

## Void a receipt

If a receipt is wrong, open it, press **Void** and give a reason. It's marked **VOID** and kept for your records.

## Reissue

After correcting what was wrong (for example the tax profile), press **Reissue**. The new receipt gets a new number. The old one says **Replaced by …** and links to the new one.

Receipts are never edited in place, so your records always show what was issued and when.

## Refunds

A full or partial refund through Stripe is shown on the payment. A partly refunded payment shows the refund and the net amount.
