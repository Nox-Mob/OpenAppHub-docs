## Your join page

Every organization has a join page at `/join/your-organization`. Find it on the **Overview** under **Share your join page**, with **Copy** and **Open** buttons. Put the link on your website, in newsletters or on a flyer.

Until payments are connected, the card says online joining opens once payments are connected, with a link to Settings → Payments.

## What a new member does

1. Opens the join page and picks a **level** and **term**.
2. Signs in or creates an account when asked.
3. Answers **About you**: every required member field must be filled in. If you use pricing rules, the price updates as they answer.
4. Pays through Stripe's checkout.
5. Returns to your app. The return page waits for the payment to be confirmed, then shows the result.

If a card is declined or they cancel, they see a clear message and no membership is created. They can try again.

By joining, members agree to the terms and privacy policy linked on the join page.

## Approval

If your organization approves new members (**Settings → General**), a paid application waits as **Pending**. Approve or decline it under **Members → Applications**. Declining a paid application refunds the payment.

## Renewing

Members renew from their **Overview**: **Your membership → Renew online**. The new term starts the day after the current paid-through date, even when they renew early.

Members see a reminder in **Actions needed** when their membership is ending soon.

## Two-level dues and home chapters

- If the national requires its own membership, chapter join pages take two payments. See [Two-level dues](/charter/membership/two-level-dues/).
- In the home-chapter model, chapter join pages send people to the national's page. See [National membership with a home chapter](/charter/membership/home-chapter/).

## On phones

The join page and checkout work on phones.
