There are two places:

- **Board** shows who holds each seat and lets you **appoint** and **end terms**.
- **Settings → Board settings** is where seats are defined: terms, titles, contact emails and board rules.

Administrators see **Change board settings** on the Board page. Members see the Board but can't change it.

## Add a seat

**Settings → Board settings → Add seat.** Choose the name, where it's used (this organization, every chapter below, or both), whether it's **required**, a suggested term (or **No fixed term**), and optionally a **linked role**.

Required seats with nobody in them show as **Vacant**, with a warning on the Board page and in Reports.

## Appoint an officer

**Board** → the seat → **Appoint**. Pick a member. The dialog shows when the term will end.

By default the person must be a member in **good standing**. Someone lapsed or cancelled is refused with a clear message.

If the seat has a **linked role**, the person gets that role on appointment.

## End a term

**Board** → **End term**, with a reason. The term moves to **Past officers**, with the reason. A linked role is removed unless they also had it by hand.

Terms also end when a member is removed from the roster, or changes home chapter.

## Change a seat's term

**Settings → Board settings** → the seat → **Change term**. Enter the length (or **No fixed term**), the date it takes effect and the by-law reference.

- The seat shows "Term: 1 year (Bylaws Art. IV §2, since …)".
- **Term history** keeps every past rule.
- When asked **Apply to current officers?**, OK moves the current holder's end date to match.

## Rename a seat in a chapter

At a chapter: **Settings → Board settings** → the seat → **Rename here**, for example "Commander" instead of "President". Only that chapter's display name changes. A national can turn off renaming in **Board rules**.

## Contact emails

Each seat shows a contact to members:

- **A role email for this seat** (for example president@yourclub.org). It stays the same when officers change.
- **The officer's sign-in email.**

Set it at **Settings → Board settings** → the seat → **Contact email**. A vacant seat with a role email is still listed, as **Vacant**, so members can reach it.
