**Settings → Setup → Board setup** defines your officer seats and their terms in one guided flow. Run it again whenever your by-laws change.

## Steps

1. **Officer seats.** Tick the seats you have (for example President, Treasurer). For each, choose where it's used:
   - **the organization itself**,
   - **every chapter**, or
   - **the organization and every chapter** (each fills its own).

   Mark seats **required** to get vacancy warnings, and set a suggested term (for example 2 years). Add your own seats, such as Chaplain.
2. **Officer rules** (nationals). For example, **May chapters add their own seats?** and whether chapters may rename seats.
3. **Terms from your by-laws.** For any seat, tick **set our own term**, enter the length, the date it takes effect and the by-law reference (for example "Bylaws Art. II").
4. **Review** the summary, then **Apply setup**.

## Running it again

Applying with no changes changes nothing. Applying with a change only updates that change.

You can't change a seat from "organization and every chapter" back to "the organization itself" while chapters still have someone in it; the app explains why.

## At a chapter

A chapter's Board setup has no seats step, because the national manages seats. Chapters can set **their own term** for a seat from their own by-laws. Other chapters keep the suggested term.

## Changing one seat at a time

You can also edit seats directly on **Settings → Board settings**. See [Board seats and officers](/charter/governance/seats-and-officers/).
