Under **Board settings**, define the board's seats (for one organization, its chapters, or both) and the terms
from your by-laws. Appointing someone to a seat gives them that seat's role; ending the appointment removes it.
Each seat has a contact: a role email or the officer's own.

## With OpenBoardApp

If your organization also uses OpenBoardApp, the officers of your top level (the association, or the national)
appear in OpenBoardApp's members and terms. Ending a term there ends the appointment here. Chapter officers stay
in OpenCharterApp.

## Chapters on OpenBoardApp

A chapter can run its own board in OpenBoardApp (meetings, agendas, minutes, votes and action items) if its
national allows it.

**National:** under **Board settings → Chapters on OpenBoardApp**, choose:

- **Chapters ask, and you approve** (the default): you get an email when a chapter asks; approve or decline it
  on the same page.
- **Any chapter can turn it on.**
- **Chapters can't use OpenBoardApp.**

**Chapter:** on the chapter's **Board** page, an admin selects **Ask to use OpenBoardApp** (or **Use
OpenBoardApp** if no approval is needed). Once it's on, **Open OpenBoardApp** takes you to the chapter's board.

The chapter gets its own organization in OpenAppHub, under the national's. Whoever asked owns it, and the
national's owner is an admin. The chapter's officers appear on its board, and stay in step as officers change.
It's covered by the national's plan: the chapter has no bill of its own.
