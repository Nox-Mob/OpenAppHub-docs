---
title: "Board seats"
description: "Board seats, terms and appointments."
sidebar:
  order: 1
---

Under **Board settings**, define the board's seats (for one organization, its chapters, or both) and the terms
from your by-laws. Appointing someone to a seat gives them that seat's role; ending the appointment removes it.
Each seat has a contact: a role email or the officer's own.
