Each organization's **Overview** is its home page. What it shows depends on who you are.

## For administrators: At a glance

| Tile | What it counts |
|---|---|
| Current members | People with a membership in good standing or grace |
| Chapters (nationals) | Chapters below you |
| Average members per chapter (nationals) | Current members ÷ chapters |
| Lifetime members | With the share of all current members |
| New in the last 30 days | New memberships |
| Waiting for approval | Pending applications |
| Renewals due in 30 days | Memberships ending soon |
| Revenue this year | Paid, minus refunds; test payments excluded |
| Vacant required seats | Required board seats with nobody in them |

A person who's a member of both the national and a chapter counts once. **See each chapter on the Reports page** opens Reports.

Below the tiles is **Share your join page**.

## For members

- **Actions needed:** things to do, such as renew, finish an application, complete required profile fields, pick a chapter, or a board term ending within 30 days. Or "You're all set".
- **Your membership:** level, standing, paid-through date, **Renew online** and **Your receipts**.
- **Your profile:** your name, email and the member fields you may see. **Edit** shows only the fields members may change.
- **Your roles and seats.**
- **Board and who to contact.**
- **Show me in the member directory.**

## View as member

Administrators can press **View as member** next to the organization name to preview the member view. The menu shrinks to what members see, a banner explains it's a preview, and the Overview shows your own member cards. Press **Exit member view** to go back.

The preview only changes what's on screen; your administrator access is unchanged.
