**Reports** is for administrators. A national sees every chapter; a chapter sees only itself, "as of today".

## Members

One row per organization (chapters indented under the national) and a **Total**.

- **Members** counts people with a current membership (good standing or grace), not everyone on the Members page.
- The **standing bar** shows good, grace, lapsed and pending. Hover a segment for the count and share.
- In the home-chapter model, the Total equals the national's own row, because each member is counted once even though they also appear in their chapter.

## Revenue

Defaults to **this year so far**; change the dates as needed. Test payments aren't counted. Partly refunded payments show the refund and the net.

## Boards

- **Required seats with nobody in them**, listed by chapter.
- **Terms past their end:** officers still serving after their planned end date.

## Export

Each section has **Export CSV**. Exports are recorded in the Audit log.

## On phones and in dark mode

Tables scroll sideways inside their box on small screens. Standing colours stay readable in dark mode.
