## Reports

The **Reports** tab has three sections. Each person sees the sections their role allows, for what they manage: a national sees its whole tree (regions and chapters), a chapter admin only their chapter.

| Section | Shows | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| **Members** | Current members, by standing (good, grace, lapsed) and by level, per chapter, with totals | People who can see members |
| **Revenue** | Payments received in the date range you choose (**From**, **To**), per chapter and in total | People who can see payments |
| **Boards** | Each unit's board seats: filled, vacant, and terms ending soon | People who can see the organization's board |

**Download reports (PDF)** saves the sections you see as one PDF, to keep or to share.
- **Audit log** (under **Settings**): each change reads "time, who did what", with the previous and new value
  of every field, one entry per save.

## Add to a board package

If your organization uses OpenBoardApp, **Add to a board package** next to **Download reports (PDF)** sends the
report as a PDF to an upcoming meeting. It goes into that meeting's documents and its board packet.

## Export everything

**Settings** > **General** > **Export everything** downloads every member, membership, level, payment, receipt, board seat and setting for your organization (and, on a national, its chapters) as a zip: one spreadsheet (CSV) per table, plus one JSON file with everything. It works even when the app is read-only (for example after your plan ends), so your records are always yours.
