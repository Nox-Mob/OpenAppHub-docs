Only a **national** has chapters. Chapters belong to their national (or to a region under it), and the national's rules apply to them.

## Add a chapter

As a national administrator: **Chapters** → **Add chapter**. The wizard asks step by step:

1. **Name and type.** Choose **Chapter** or **Region**. A region skips the chapter-only steps.
2. **Chapter details.** The national's chapter fields, such as charter date. Required fields must be filled in. See [Member and chapter fields](/charter/setup/member-and-chapter-fields/).
3. **Chapter admin.** An email address to invite as the chapter's administrator, or leave it blank.
4. **Required officers.** Only if the national requires seats in every chapter. An email per seat, or blank.

Press **Create**. You'll see the **invitation links** to copy and send. **Open …** goes to the new chapter.

The new chapter appears in the Chapters list and the organization switcher. Its **Board** shows required seats as **Vacant** until someone is appointed.

:::tip
If you enter the same email for the admin and an officer, the app makes one invitation. If an email is badly formed, the wizard stops before creating anything.
:::

## After the chapter accepts

1. The invited admin opens their link and accepts.
2. An invited officer accepts too. Appoint them on the chapter's **Board**. By default, officers must hold a membership in good standing first; see [Board seats and officers](/charter/governance/seats-and-officers/).

## What a chapter can and can't change

The national decides, for each setting, whether chapters **follow** it, may **change** it, or are **locked** to it. See [Settings and locks](/charter/setup/settings-and-locks/).

## Chapters in reports

The national's **Reports** and **Members** pages include every chapter. A chapter administrator sees only their own chapter.
