Owners and admins change these under the organization's **Settings** > **General** (the menu on the left). Some apply only to a national (or region) and what its chapters may do; a few are on **Board settings** instead.

## Membership

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **New members need admin approval** | When on, join requests wait for an admin before the person becomes a member | Off |
| **How new members of your chapters are accepted** (national) | **Accepted automatically** or **Always reviewed by an admin** applies to every chapter; **Each chapter decides** lets each chapter set the one above | Each chapter decides |
| **Grace period (days)** | How long after a membership expires the member keeps their standing (for example, still counts as in good standing when appointed to a board seat) | 30 |
| **Chapters may create their own membership levels** (national) | Whether chapters can add levels of their own | On |
| **Do chapters sell their own memberships?** (national) | **No: chapters use your levels and dues**, **Each chapter may choose**, or **Yes: every chapter sells its own** | Each chapter may choose |
| **Chapter members also need a membership with you** (national) | A chapter membership also requires a national one | Off |
| **Lowest / Highest chapter dues (in cents)** (national) | Limits on what chapters may charge for a term. 0 means no limit. Example: 2500 is $25.00 | 0 |

## Members and privacy

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Members are listed in the member directory** | The default for members who haven't chosen. Each member can opt in or out | On |
| **Chapter admins may export members who opted out** (national) | When off, members who chose not to be listed are always left out of chapter exports | On |

## Receipts and contact

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Receipt footer** | Printed at the bottom of every receipt, for example a thank-you or your address | Empty |
| **Tax-exempt organization** | On: each receipt follows its membership level's tax treatment. Off: every receipt says the payment is not tax-deductible ([Payments and receipts](/charter/money/payments/#receipts)) | On |
| **Public contact email** | Shown on your public join page and on receipts | Empty |
| **Tax profile** | Legal name, EIN, tax status (for example 501(c)(3)) and address, printed on receipts | Empty |

Receipt settings apply to receipts issued from then on; issued receipts never change.

## Board (on Board settings)

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Officers must be members in good standing** | Checked when someone is appointed to a board seat | On |
| **Chapters may rename the board seats you define** (national) | When off, chapters show your seat titles (for example always "President") | On |
| **Chapters may add their own board seats** (national) | When off, chapters use only the seats you define | On |
| **Chapters on OpenBoardApp** (national) | Whether chapters may run their own board in OpenBoardApp, with your approval, freely, or not at all ([Chapters' own organizations](/hub/accounts/chapters/)) | Chapters ask, and you approve |

## Locked settings

A national can lock a setting so its chapters follow it; chapters then see it with a lock and can't change it. The membership model (separate memberships, or a national membership with a home chapter) is chosen once and can't be changed ([Chapters, regions and your join page](/charter/setup/chapters-and-join-page/#how-membership-works-across-chapters)).
