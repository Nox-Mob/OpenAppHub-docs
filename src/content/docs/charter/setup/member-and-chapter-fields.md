**Fields** are the extra information you keep, such as a member's rank, branch or salutation, or a chapter's charter date.

- **Member fields:** Settings → **Member fields**
- **Chapter fields** (nationals): Settings → **Chapter fields**

## Add a field

Press **Add field**, then choose its name and type. A **choice list** can have as many options as you need; the dialog scrolls while its title and Save stay in view.

For each member field you can also decide:

- whether it's **required** (the join page asks for it, and members are reminded to fill it in)
- whether **members may edit** it on their own profile, or only admins can
- whether it's **shown in the directory**

## Ordered lists

An **ordered list** is a choice list where the order matters, such as ranks E-1 to E-9. Ordered lists let you say "at least" or "at most":

- in member filters ("Rank is at least E-5")
- in [pricing rules](/charter/membership/pricing-rules/)

## Changing and retiring fields

Fields and their options are **retired**, never deleted, so the values already stored keep their meaning. A retired option stops being offered for new entries.

## Locking chapter fields

A national can lock a chapter field so chapters see it read-only. See [Settings and locks](/charter/setup/settings-and-locks/).

## Using fields

- Show them as **columns** on the Members page (Members → **Columns**).
- Filter and export by them.
- Map them when [importing members](/charter/members/importing/).
