## Settings → General

Here you set your organization's basics:

- name and general **contact email** (shown to members under "Board and who to contact")
- **how membership works** (nationals; see [Choosing how membership works](/charter/membership/membership-models/))
- whether new members need **approval**
- the **grace period**: days after a membership's paid-through date during which the member keeps good standing
- **rules for chapters** (nationals)
- the **tax profile** and **receipt footer** (see [Tax profile, receipts and branding](/charter/setup/branding-and-tax-profile/))
- the **logo**

## How a national's settings reach its chapters

Every setting at a national applies to its chapters in one of three ways:

| Mode | What it means for chapters |
|---|---|
| **Inherit** | Chapters follow the national's value |
| **Override** | Chapters may set their own value |
| **Locked** | Chapters must use the national's value and can't change it |

A locked setting shows as read-only at the chapter, with **Locked by a parent organization**.

Permissions and settings only flow **down**. A chapter can never change its national's settings.

## Rules for chapters (separate-memberships model)

A national using separate memberships answers:

- **Do chapters sell their own memberships?** No (chapters use your levels and dues), Each chapter may choose, or Yes (every chapter sells its own).
- **Chapter members also need a membership with you?** If yes, joining a chapter takes two payments. See [Two-level dues](/charter/membership/two-level-dues/).
- A **chapter price range**, so chapter prices stay between a lowest and highest amount.

Board rules (renaming seats, extra seats, officers in good standing) are in **Settings → Board settings**, not here.

## Settings that don't apply

In the **national membership with a home chapter** model, settings that only make sense when chapters have their own members (chapter levels, chapter dues, chapter approval) are hidden. See [National membership with a home chapter](/charter/membership/home-chapter/).
