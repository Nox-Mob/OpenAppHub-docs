A **role** is a named set of permissions, such as "see member profiles" or "record payments". People get permissions by being given roles.

## Manage roles

**Settings → Roles.**

- **Add role:** name it (for example "Newsletter editor") and tick its permissions.
- **Assign a role** to a member, or remove it.

## Roles at a national and its chapters

- A role defined at the national is offered to its chapters too.
- Permissions only flow **down**: a national administrator can act in its chapters, but a chapter administrator can't act in the national.
- You can only create or give a role with permissions **you hold yourself**. Others aren't offered.

## Roles from board seats

A board seat can be linked to a role. Appointing someone to the seat gives them the role; ending their term takes it away, unless they also had it by hand. See [Board seats and officers](/charter/governance/seats-and-officers/).

## Safety rules

- An organization's **last administrator can't be removed.** Add another administrator first.
- You can't remove yourself from the roster, or remove someone who holds a role given on Settings → Roles. Take the role away first.

## Admins versus members

Administrators see Members, Reports and Settings. Members see their own Overview, the Directory and the Board. To check what members see, use **View as member**; see [The Overview](/charter/records/overview/).
