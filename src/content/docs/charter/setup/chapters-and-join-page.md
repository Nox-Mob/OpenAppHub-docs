## Standalone or national

When you set up an organization in OpenCharterApp, you choose how it's organized. You can't change this later.

| Type                                  | Use it when                                       | You get                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Standalone** (a single association) | You run one group of members with one set of dues | Members, levels, dues, payments, officers and reports for one organization. It **can't add chapters later**, so choose national if you might                             |
| **National** (with chapters)          | You have local groups (chapters, posts, lodges)   | Chapters, each with its own members, officers, dues and join page; optional **regions** to group chapters; reports that roll up from chapters to regions to the national |

If your organization was created in OpenAppHub, OpenCharterApp asks this the first time you open it: **Set up as an association** or **Set up as a national with chapters**. Only the organization's owner or an admin can choose.

## Adding a chapter or region

On your national's page, select **Add chapter** (or add a region, then chapters under it). The wizard asks:

| Field                       | What it's for                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Name**                    | The chapter's name, for example _Post 12 Austin_                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Web address**             | The address of the chapter's **public join page**, where people find it, sign up and pay dues. It's made from the name (_post-12-austin_) and shown in full under the field, for example `charter.openapphub.org/join/post-12-austin`. You can change it now: lowercase letters, numbers and dashes. If it turns red with _That web address is taken_, another organization already uses it; try another. Changing it later breaks links you've already shared |
| **Type**                    | **Chapter** (a local group with its own members, officers, dues and join page; its national sees its reports) or **Region** (groups chapters by area; chapters can sit under it and the region sees their reports)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Chapter details**         | Any chapter fields your national defined (for example a charter number); required ones are marked with \*                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Who runs it? (optional)** | An email to invite as the chapter's first admin. You can do it later. Once they've joined, appoint them to a seat on the chapter's **Board** tab                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |

New chapters show their required board seats as **Vacant** until you appoint officers.

## How membership works across chapters

A national chooses one **membership model**, once, for every chapter (in the national's setup, or the first time you add a chapter):

| Model                                       | How people belong                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Separate memberships**                    | Each chapter sells its own memberships. A person can belong to several chapters, each with its own dues. The national may also require a national membership (**Chapter members also need a membership with you**) |
| **National membership with a home chapter** | People join the national and pick a **home chapter**. One membership, one set of dues; chapters don't have levels of their own                                                                                     |

Pick the model your by-laws describe. Your national's **Settings** then decide how much chapters may do themselves: whether they sell their own memberships, the lowest and highest dues they may charge, and whether new members need approval (see [Settings reference](/charter/setup/settings/)).

## Your join page

Every organization and chapter has a public join page at its web address. People there can:

- read who you are and what each membership level costs
- pick a chapter (on a national's page) or go to a chapter's own page
- answer your member fields (for example rank or branch) and pay online with a card, once your [payment connection](/charter/money/payments/) is set up
- if approval is on, send a join request that an admin approves

Find the link to share on **Payments** (**Your join page**, with **Copy**), or on the organization's overview. Put it on your website, in emails and on flyers. To join, people sign in, or create an account with their email, right on the join page; it takes a minute and they need it to renew and to see their membership later.

## Chapters running their own board

A chapter can also run its board in OpenBoardApp, with its officers already there. Your national decides whether chapters may (**Board settings** > **Chapters on OpenBoardApp**). How it works: [Chapters' own organizations](/hub/accounts/chapters/).
