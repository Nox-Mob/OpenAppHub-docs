All three are on **Settings → General**.

## Tax profile

Your legal name, EIN and tax status appear on every receipt.

1. Enter the legal name and EIN in the form `12-3456789`.
2. Press **Save tax profile**.

A badly formed EIN is refused with "EIN must look like 12-3456789", and nothing is saved.

Each organization (the national and every chapter) has its own tax profile, because each one collects its own money and issues its own receipts.

:::caution
Receipt tax wording is a placeholder until it has been reviewed by a CPA. Check it with your accountant before relying on it for tax purposes.
:::

## Receipt footer

Add a thank-you note or other text to the bottom of receipts. It appears on receipts issued **after** you save it; earlier receipts don't change.

## Logo

Upload an image file; a PNG with a transparent background works best. It shows on your organization's pages and receipts straight away.

At a national, chapters show the national's logo unless they upload their own and the national allows it.
