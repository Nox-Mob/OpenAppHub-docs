## Kinds of organization

| Kind | Use it for |
|---|---|
| **Standalone** | A single local association with no national above it and no chapters below it |
| **National** | A top-level organization with chapters beneath it, which sets the rules for them |
| **Region** | An optional level between a national and its chapters |
| **Chapter** | A local group under a national or region |

Chapters and regions are created from the national's **Chapters** page, not from here. See [Chapters and regions](/charter/setup/chapters/).

## Create one

1. Organization switcher → **All organizations…** → **Create organization**.
2. Enter the name and choose **Standalone** or **National**.
3. For a national, step 2 is **How membership works**. Read the two models side by side and pick one, or choose **Decide later**. See [Choosing how membership works](/charter/membership/membership-models/).
4. Press **Create**.

You become the organization's administrator.

## What to do next

Work through **Settings → Setup**. It has three cards:

1. **Membership setup:** levels, terms, prices, joining and renewals. See [Setting up membership](/charter/membership/setup-and-levels/).
2. **Board setup:** officer seats, rules and terms. See [Board setup](/charter/governance/board-setup/).
3. **Connect payments:** opens Settings → Payments. See [Connecting payments](/charter/money/payments/).

Then fill in **Settings → General** (contact email, tax profile, logo) and add your members.

## The web address (slug)

Your organization gets a short web address used in its join page, for example `/join/riverside-club`. It's suggested from the name; accents and apostrophes are simplified.

## Deleting an organization

Only **demo organizations** can be deleted from the app. A real organization's records are kept. Contact support if you need an organization closed.
