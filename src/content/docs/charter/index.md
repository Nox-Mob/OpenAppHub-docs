OpenCharterApp is a membership platform for associations. It works for a **single local group**, and for a **national organization with local chapters** (and optional regions in between), such as veterans' groups, fraternal and professional societies and clubs.

## What it does

- **Members:** a roster with standing (good, grace, lapsed, pending), member fields you define, filters, saved views, imports and exports.
- **Dues:** membership levels and terms, prices that depend on member fields, online joining and renewing, and manual payments.
- **Money:** each organization collects into **its own Stripe account** and issues its own numbered receipts. A national and each chapter keep their money separate.
- **Governance:** board seats with terms from your by-laws, appointments, vacancy warnings and contact emails per seat.
- **Nationals and chapters:** the national sets the rules; each rule can be followed, changed or locked for chapters.
- **Records:** reports, an audit log of every important change, and exports for bookkeeping.

## Who sees what

- **Administrators** see the Members page, numbers on the Overview, Reports and Settings.
- **Members** see what they need to do, their own profile and membership, the member directory and who to contact on the board.

Administrators can preview the member view at any time with **View as member**.

## Version

This documentation covers **OpenCharterApp 0.1.0**, the first release an association can run on. Some features are still to come, notably automatic email. See [Known limits](/charter/help/known-limits/).
