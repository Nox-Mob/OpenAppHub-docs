**Standing** is worked out from a member's membership. It decides who counts as a current member, who can hold office and what members are reminded about.

| Standing | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Good** | Paid through a date that hasn't passed, or a lifetime membership |
| **Grace** | The paid-through date has passed, but they're still within your grace period. They keep good standing for most purposes. |
| **Lapsed** | Past the paid-through date and the grace period |
| **Pending** | Applied and waiting for approval |

## Key dates

- **Paid-through date:** the last day of the current term.
- **Expires in:** days left until the paid-through date, shown on the member's record.
- **Grace period:** set on **Settings → General**. A national can lock it for chapters.

## Who counts as a member

In **Reports**, "Members" counts people with a **current** membership: good standing or grace. People on the Members page without a current membership (for example an administrator who never joined) aren't counted.

## Standing and the board

By default, someone must be in good standing to be appointed to a board seat. A lapsed or cancelled member is refused with a clear message. A national can change this rule in **Settings → Board settings → Board rules**.

## National standing

In the separate model, when a national requires its own membership, chapter administrators also see each member's **national standing**. See [Two-level dues](/charter/membership/two-level-dues/).

## Renewal reminders

Members see "Your membership ends on … (in N days)" with a **Renew** button in **Actions needed** on their Overview. Email reminders are planned; see [Known limits](/charter/help/known-limits/).
