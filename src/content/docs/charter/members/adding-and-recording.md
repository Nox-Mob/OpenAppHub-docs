Members can join online (see [Joining and renewing online](/charter/money/joining-and-renewing/)). Administrators can also add members and record payments by hand, for cash, checks and historical records.

## Add a member

**Members → Add member.** Enter their name and any member fields. An email is optional:

- **With an email,** you can invite them so they can sign in and see their own membership.
- **Without an email,** they're listed as **Contact (no login yet)**. You can add an email and invite them later; the invitation lets them claim the record.

To add many members at once, see [Importing members](/charter/members/importing/).

## Record a membership

Open the member → **Record membership**:

1. Choose the level and term.
2. Choose how they paid (for example check or cash).
3. Save.

The member is now in **Good** standing, **paid through** one term from the start date (a one-year membership is paid through a year less a day). The payment appears in their **History** and gets a numbered receipt.

For a historical member, set the start date in the past. Their standing reflects today: they may already be in **Grace** or **Lapsed**.

## Record a renewal

Open the member → **Record membership** again. A renewal starts **the day after** the current paid-through date, so no time is lost.

## Cancel a membership

Open the member → cancel the membership and confirm **Cancel this membership now?** The History shows it.

## Applications waiting for approval

If your organization approves new members, online applications appear under **Members → Applications**.

- **Approve** makes them a member.
- **Decline** refuses the application. If they already paid, they're refunded through Stripe, and the History says so.

## Member details

Open any member to see their profile, fields, membership, standing, roles, seats and full History.
