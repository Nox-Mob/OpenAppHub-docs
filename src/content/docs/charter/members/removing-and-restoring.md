Member records are never deleted. Instead you **take them off the roster**, which hides them from the list and keeps their history.

## Remove from the roster

1. Open the member → **Remove from roster…**
2. Give a reason → **Remove …**

What happens:

- They leave the Members list.
- Their membership shows as **cancelled** in their history.
- Any **board seat** they held ends.
- They can no longer act as a member of the organization.

## Restore

At the bottom of the Members page, **Show *N* removed from the roster** lists them. Press **Restore** to bring someone back. Their membership isn't restarted: record a new membership to make them current again.

## When you can't remove someone

- **Yourself.**
- **Someone with a role** given on Settings → Roles. Remove the role first.
- **A chapter record in the home-chapter model.** Move or remove the member at the national; removing the national record takes them off the chapter too.

## Why not delete?

Payments, receipts, board terms and the audit log all point to the member. Keeping the record keeps your organization's history accurate. If someone asks for their personal data to be erased, contact support.
