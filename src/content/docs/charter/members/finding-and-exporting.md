Everything here is on the **Members** page toolbar.

## Search

Type part of a name or email. Accents don't matter: "jose" finds "José".

## Filter

Filter by **standing**, **level**, any **member field** (ordered lists support "at least"), or **Expiring within 30 / 60 / 90 days**. The page shows **Showing *x* of *y***.

## Saved views

Set up your search and filters, then **Save this view**. Click it any time to get the same list back.

## Columns

**Columns** lists your member fields. Tick the ones to show as columns (for example Rank and Branch). Your choice is remembered in this browser, per organization.

## Everyone across chapters (nationals)

A national's Members page opens on **Everyone in *National* and below**: members of every chapter, with an **Organization** column and an **Open** button that goes to that chapter's Members page.

Switch to **Only *National*** to work with the national's own members, roles and details.

A chapter administrator sees only their chapter's members.

## Export to CSV

**Export CSV** downloads the list as you see it, with your chosen columns. It opens cleanly in Excel and Google Sheets: accents, dates and empty cells are kept, and nothing in the file runs as a formula.

- From **Everyone in … and below**, the file has an **Organization** column.
- Members who **opted out of the directory** are left out unless you tick **Include … who opted out**, if your national allows it. Each chapter's own default applies to its rows.
- Every export is recorded in the **Audit log**, including whether it covered the organizations below.

## Large rosters

Lists with thousands of members load in full. Searching and filtering stay fast with tens of thousands of members.
