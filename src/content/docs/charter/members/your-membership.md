This page is for **members**: what you see when you sign in to your organization in OpenCharterApp.

## Your membership card

**Your membership** shows your level, your standing and when your membership ends:

| You see | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Active, with an end date | You're a member in good standing until then |
| **Lifetime membership** | It never ends |
| Ending soon: *Renew below to keep it* | Renew now; the time is added from your current end date, so nothing is lost |
| Lapsed: *Renew below to restart it* | Your membership ended; renewing starts a new term |
| *Contact the organization to renew* | Your organization doesn't take renewals online for your level |
| *An admin will review your application* | You've applied and are waiting for approval ([Reviewing join requests](/charter/membership/join-requests/)) |

## Renewing

Where your organization takes payments online, renew from your card: choose the term, pay, and your card updates as soon as the payment goes through. You get an email receipt.

## Your receipts

**Your receipts** lists every receipt for your payments. Open one to view or print it.

## The member directory

**Show me in the member directory** chooses whether other members can see you in the directory. Your organization sets the default; your choice overrides it. Turning it off also leaves you out of chapter exports unless your national allows chapter admins to include everyone ([Members](/charter/members/roster/#the-member-directory-and-privacy)).
