## The directory

Members see a **Directory** of other members (administrators use the Members page instead).

The directory **never shows email addresses**. It shows names and only the member fields your organization marked "show in directory".

## Opting out

On your **Overview**, turn off **Show me in the member directory**. You disappear from the directory straight away.

Administrators still see you on the Members page, marked **Not in directory**, because they need the full roster to run the organization.

## Exports and opt-outs

Member exports leave out people who opted out of the directory, unless an administrator ticks **Include … who opted out** and the national allows it. See [Finding and exporting members](/charter/members/finding-and-exporting/).

## Board contacts

Members see "Board and who to contact" on the Overview. A seat with a **role email** (for example president@…) shows that instead of the officer's personal email. See [Board seats and officers](/charter/governance/seats-and-officers/).

## What administrators can see

Administrators can see members' profiles, contact details, memberships and payments, because that's needed to run the organization. Every export and important change they make is recorded in the audit log.
