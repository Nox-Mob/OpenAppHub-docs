**National**: the top of the organization, with chapters (and sometimes regions) under it.

**Chapter**: a local unit of a national. It runs its own members and money.

**Home chapter**: the chapter a member of a national belongs to.

**Standing**: whether a membership is current: good standing, grace period or lapsed.

**Membership level**: a kind of membership, with its own term and price.

**Web address** (slug): the last part of an organization's or chapter's public join page address, for example `post-12-austin` in `charter.openapphub.org/join/post-12-austin`. Made from the name; lowercase letters, numbers and dashes.

**Join page**: the public page where people find your organization or chapter, choose a membership and pay. See [Chapters, regions and your join page](/charter/setup/chapters-and-join-page/).

**Level** and **term**: a kind of membership (Regular, Family, Life) and how long it lasts and what it costs (1 year for $40). See [Membership and dues](/charter/membership/levels-and-dues/).

**Pricing rule**: a different price for members who answer a member field a certain way; the first rule that fits sets the price.

**Grace period**: how long after a membership expires a member keeps their standing (30 days unless you change it).

**Tax treatment**: what a level's receipts say about taxes (tax-deductible, partly, or not).
