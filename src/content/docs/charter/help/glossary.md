---

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Organization** | Any association in the app: standalone, national, region or chapter |
| **Standalone** | A single association with no national above it and no chapters below |
| **National** | A top-level organization with chapters, which sets rules for them |
| **Region** | An optional level between a national and its chapters |
| **Chapter** | A local group under a national or region |
| **Membership model** | How a national handles membership: separate memberships, or national membership with a home chapter |
| **Home chapter** | In the home-chapter model, the chapter a national member chooses to belong to |
| **At-large chapter** | A chapter for members without a local one, such as an online chapter |
| **Level** | A kind of membership, such as Regular or Lifetime |
| **Term** | A length and price option for a level, such as 1 year for $50. A term with no length is lifetime |
| **Paid-through date** | The last day of the current term |
| **Grace period** | Days after the paid-through date during which a member keeps good standing |
| **Standing** | Good, grace, lapsed or pending, worked out from the membership |
| **Stacked renewal** | Renewing early: the new term starts after the current one ends |
| **Pricing rule** | A different price when a member's fields match a condition |
| **Suggested level** | A level a national offers to chapters to copy or link |
| **Copy / Link** | Copy: the chapter's own editable level. Link: follows the national's level, with the chapter's price relative to it |
| **Two-level dues** | Joining a chapter also requires joining the national: two payments, two receipts |
| **National standing** | A chapter member's standing in the national's membership |
| **Member field / chapter field** | Information you choose to keep, such as rank or charter date |
| **Ordered list** | A field whose options have an order, such as ranks, so rules can say "at least" |
| **Inherit / override / locked** | How a national's setting applies to chapters: followed, changeable, or unchangeable |
| **Seat** | A board position, such as President |
| **Required seat** | A seat that must always be filled; vacancies are flagged |
| **Appointment** | A person holding a seat for a period |
| **Role email** | A contact address for a seat, such as president@…, that stays when officers change |
| **Role** | A named set of permissions given to people |
| **Contact (no login yet)** | A member record without a sign-in |
| **Remove from roster** | Taking a member off the list while keeping their history |
| **Receipt version** | A reissued receipt that replaces an earlier one, which is kept and marked replaced |
| **Void** | Marking a receipt invalid, with a reason |
| **Direct payments** | Each organization collects into its own Stripe account |
| **Tax profile** | Your legal name, EIN and tax status, used on receipts |
| **Actions needed** | A member's to-do list on the Overview |
| **View as member** | An administrator's preview of what members see |
| **Demo organization** | A practice organization with test payments that can be deleted |
| **Audit log** | The permanent record of who changed what and when |
