A **demo organization** is a practice copy for trying things out. Demo organizations show a **Demo** badge, never send email, and can use **test payments** instead of a real Stripe account.

## Who can create one

Only platform operators see **This is a demo organization** when creating an organization. Chapters added under a demo national are demo chapters.

## Good uses

- Trying the **membership model** you're considering. The choice can't be undone, so try it in a demo national first.
- Training a new treasurer or membership secretary.
- Practising imports before importing your real roster.

## Test payments

**Settings → Payments → Use test payments (demo only).** The checkout page offers buttons to simulate: card accepted, declined, insufficient funds, cancelled, and accepted with slow confirmation.

## Deleting a demo organization

**Settings → General → Delete this demo organization.** **Delete for good** stays disabled until you type the organization's exact name. Its chapters are deleted with it. The accounts you used aren't affected.

A real organization's Settings never show this option, and a demo chapter can't be deleted on its own; delete from the top.
