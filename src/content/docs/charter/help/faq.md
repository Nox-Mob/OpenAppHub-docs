**Can each chapter collect its own dues?**
Yes. Each level collects its own money into its own Stripe account and issues its own receipts.

**Can a national stop chapters changing a setting?**
Yes. Settings can be inherited, overridden or locked.

**Do dues-paying members count toward an OpenAppHub plan?**
No. Only the people who run the organization do.
