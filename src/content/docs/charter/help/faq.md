## Members and dues

**Can a member belong to both the national and a chapter?**
Yes. In the separate-memberships model they hold two memberships; in the home-chapter model they hold one national membership and pick a home chapter. See [Choosing how membership works](/charter/membership/membership-models/).

**Can we change the membership model later?**
No. Choose **Decide later** if you're unsure.

**Does renewing early waste the remaining time?**
No. The new term starts the day after the current paid-through date.

**Can we charge veterans, seniors or students a different price?**
Yes, with [pricing rules](/charter/membership/pricing-rules/) based on member fields.

**Can we offer lifetime memberships?**
Yes: a term with no length is lifetime.

**Can we bill members later instead of collecting up front?**
Not yet. Postpaid dues and invoices are planned.

**Can members pay by PayPal?**
Not yet. Stripe is supported; PayPal is planned.

## Money

**Where does the money go?**
Straight into your organization's own Stripe account. A national and each chapter use their own accounts.

**Does the national take a cut of chapter dues?**
Not through the app. Each organization collects its own payments.

**Are receipts valid for tax deductions?**
Receipts include your legal name, EIN and tax wording, but the wording hasn't yet been reviewed by a CPA. Check it with your accountant.

**How do I correct a receipt?**
Void it with a reason, fix what was wrong, and reissue. See [Receipts](/charter/money/receipts/).

## People and access

**Why didn't the new member get an email?**
The app doesn't send email yet. Copy the invitation link and send it yourself.

**Someone left. Can I delete them?**
Take them off the roster instead. Their history is kept and you can restore them. See [Removing and restoring members](/charter/members/removing-and-restoring/).

**Can a chapter officer see other chapters?**
No. Chapter administrators see their own chapter. National administrators see everything below them.

**How do I see what members see?**
Press **View as member** on the Overview.

## Organization

**Can we use our own web address?**
Not yet. Custom domains are planned.

**Can we delete our organization?**
Only demo organizations can be deleted in the app. Contact support for a real organization.
