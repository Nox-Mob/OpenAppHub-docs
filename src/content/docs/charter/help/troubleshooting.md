| Problem                                        | What to do                                                                |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A member can't see the organization's settings | Settings are for admins. Check their role under **Roles**.                |
| A chapter can't change a setting               | The national may have locked it.                                          |
| A spreadsheet import is refused                | The import is checked before saving; fix the rows it lists and try again. |
