## The sidebar

- **At the top:** the **organization switcher**. Pick an organization to open it. Its last entry, **All organizations…**, opens the list of your organizations and **Create organization**.
- **In the middle:** the menu for the open organization.
- **At the bottom:** **Account** and **Sign out**.

There's no separate dashboard. Each organization's **Overview** is its dashboard.

On a phone, the menu becomes a **Section** dropdown at the top of the page, and Account and Sign out move below the page.

## The organization's menu

| Section | What it's for | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| **Overview** | Numbers at a glance for admins; your to-do list and membership for members | Everyone |
| **Members** | The full roster: search, filter, record payments, import and export | Administrators |
| **Directory** | Other members who chose to be listed | Members |
| **Board** | Who holds each seat; appoint and end terms | Everyone (only admins can make changes) |
| **Reports** | Members, revenue and board reports | Administrators |
| **Chapters** | The chapters (and regions) below a national | Nationals |
| **Settings** | Everything that configures the organization | Administrators |

A simple rule: **everything that configures your organization is under Settings.** The other pages show information or let you do something.

## Settings pages

Click **Settings** to see its pages (on a phone, a second **Settings page** dropdown appears):

- **General:** name, contact email, how membership works, approval, grace period, rules for chapters, tax profile, receipt footer, logo
- **Setup:** guided setup for membership and the board, and connecting payments
- **Membership levels:** levels, terms, prices and pricing rules
- **Member fields** and **Chapter fields:** the information you keep
- **Board settings:** seats, terms, titles, contact emails and board rules
- **Roles:** who can do what
- **Payments:** Stripe connection, payments list and export
- **Audit log:** every important change

## Moving between a chapter and its national

A chapter's page shows "← *National name*" at the top. Click it to go up to the national.

## Light and dark

The app follows your device's light or dark setting.
