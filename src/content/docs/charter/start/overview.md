OpenCharterApp runs your membership. It keeps your list of members, lets people join and renew online, collects dues, gives receipts, and keeps track of your chapters and your board seats.

It works for a single organization, and for a national organization with chapters and regions under it.

## The menu

The menu on the left has these parts:

| Menu                           | What it's for                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Overview**                   | Your home page in OpenCharterApp. Admins see what needs doing, like join requests to review, and the numbers for the organizations they manage. Members see their own membership. |
| **Members** (or **Directory**) | Your list of members: their memberships, whether they're paid up (their _standing_), and their member record.                                                                     |
| **Board**                      | Your board seats, like _President_ and _Treasurer_, who holds each one, and when their terms end.                                                                                 |
| **Reports**                    | Reports for your organization or your chapter, like membership counts and dues received.                                                                                          |
| **Chapters**                   | The chapters and regions under a national organization.                                                                                                                           |
| **Settings**                   | Everything that sets up your organization: membership levels and prices, your join page, payments, member questions and more.                                                     |

## Who sees what

What you see depends on your role:

- **Admins** see everything for the organizations they manage.
- **Chapter admins** see their own chapter.
- **Members** see what they need: anything they need to do (like renew), their own profile, their chapter, their roles, and who to contact with questions.

## Where to start

New to OpenCharterApp? The [guided setup](/charter/setup/guided-setup/) walks you through the basics: your membership levels, your dues, and your join page. Then [connect Stripe](/charter/money/payments/#connect-stripe) to take dues online.
