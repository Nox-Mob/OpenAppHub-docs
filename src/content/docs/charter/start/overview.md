The menu on the left has:

| Menu                           | What's there                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Overview**                   | What needs doing, and the numbers for the organizations you manage. Members see their own membership. |
| **Members** (or **Directory**) | Your roster: memberships, standing and member records.                                                |
| **Board**                      | Board seats, terms and who holds them.                                                                |
| **Reports**                    | Reports for your organization, or your chapter.                                                       |
| **Chapters**                   | The chapters and regions under a national.                                                            |
| **Settings**                   | Everything that configures the organization.                                                          |

Admins see everything for the organizations they manage; chapter admins see their chapter. Members see what
they need to do, their profile, their chapter, their roles and who to contact.
