## Signing in

Open the sign-in page and use your email and password, or **Continue with Google**.

After signing in you land on:

1. your **default organization**, if you've chosen one; otherwise
2. your only organization, if you belong to just one; otherwise
3. the list of your organizations.

If the app says "Email or password is incorrect", check both. For privacy, it never says which one is wrong.

## Forgot your password

On the sign-in page choose **Forgot password?**, enter your email and press **Send reset link**. The message is the same whether or not the email has an account.

## Accepting an invitation

An administrator sends you an invitation link. Open it, sign in or create an account, then press the button to accept. Links work once and expire after 14 days. See [Invitations](/hub/accounts/invitations/) if a link doesn't work.

## Your account page

Sidebar → **Account** (on a phone, Account is below the page).

| What | How |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Change it and save. Your member records show the new name. |
| **Email** | **Change email** sends a confirmation link to the new address. The change happens when you open that link; until then the page says it's waiting. |
| **Password** | Change it here. If you signed up with Google, there's no password section. |
| **Default organization** | Pick the organization that opens after you sign in, or "The list of my organizations". |
| **Organizations** | The organizations you belong to. |

## Signing out

Sidebar → **Sign out**.
