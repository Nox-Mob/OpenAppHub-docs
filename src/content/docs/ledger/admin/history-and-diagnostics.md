## History

**Settings → History** shows who changed what and when. Filter by type:

- **Money:** postings, voids, imports, statement checks
- **Setup and settings:** accounts, funds, members, settings
- **System:** backups, closes and reopenings

Each entry shows the **saved change**: exactly what the database recorded.

Every change and its history entry are saved together. If either fails, neither is kept. Nobody can edit or delete history entries.

The history viewer is in the web app only for now.

## Diagnostics

**Settings → Diagnostics** (admins and treasurers) shows:

- the app version and edition (web or desktop)
- book health checks
- a **copyable report** with **no names or amounts**

Paste the report into an email to [support@openapphub.org](mailto:support@openapphub.org) when asking for help.
