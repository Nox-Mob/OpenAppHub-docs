**Settings → Security** (web app).

## Two-step sign-in

Turn on two-step sign-in with an **authenticator app** (such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or 1Password). After your password, you'll enter a code from the app.

## Require it for everyone

Admins can require two-step sign-in for the whole organization. The database then hides the books from anyone who signed in without it. They'll see:

> This organization requires two-step sign-in. Turn it on in Settings, Security, then sign in again.

## Delete my account

**Delete my account** removes your sign-in. It's blocked while you **own** an organization or are its **only admin**; transfer ownership or add another admin first.

Your past changes stay in each organization's history, shown as **Deleted user**, so the books keep an accurate record.

## Good habits

- Use a unique password.
- Remove people's access as soon as they leave the role.
- Give board members who only need reports the **View only** role.
