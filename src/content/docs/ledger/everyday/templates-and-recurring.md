**Templates** saves the transactions you enter again and again (rent, payroll, a monthly transfer to savings) so they take one click. A template can also **repeat**, and OpenLedgerApp tells you when it's due.

## Making a template

Select **Templates**, then **New template**:

| Field | |
|---|---|
| **Name** | How you'll find it, such as *Monthly rent* |
| Description | What each transaction will say, such as *Rent* (required) |
| **Accounts** | Where the money comes from and where it goes: two different accounts |
| **Amount** | The usual amount; you can change it each time |
| **Repeats** | **Doesn't repeat**, **Every week**, **Every month**, **Every 3 months** or **Every year** |
| **Next due** | For a repeating one, the next date it's due |

## When a recurring one is due

Recurring templates **never post by themselves**: money only moves when a person says so. Admins choose, for the whole organization, under **When a recurring one is due** on the Templates page:

- **Add it to a Ready to review list where I post or skip it** (the default): a draft is ready to check and post.
- **Just remind me; I'll post it myself**: the due ones are listed, and you enter them as usual.

When one is due it shows at the top of **Templates**, under ready to review, and as a task:

- on your **OpenAppHub dashboard**, under **Your tasks** (for owners, admins and treasurers), with **Skip**;
- in your [task summary email](/hub/accounts/task-summary/).

For each one, **Post** it (check the amount and date first) or **Skip** it this time. Skipping posts nothing and moves it to its next due date.

## Changing and archiving

Changing a template affects future transactions only. **Archive** a template you no longer need: past transactions made from it are unchanged.
