An **account** is a bucket that money is counted in. There are five kinds:

| Kind (Simple wording) | Accounting term | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Accounts | Assets | Checking, savings, petty cash, money owed to you |
| Debts | Liabilities | Credit card, loan |
| Owner's Equity / Net Assets | Equity | What's left after debts |
| Income | Revenue | Sales, donations, dues, grants |
| Expenses | Expenses | Rent, supplies, insurance |

Every transaction moves money between at least two accounts, for example from **Income: Donations** into **Checking**.

## Opening balances

When you start, enter what each bank, cash and card account held on your start date, from your statements. This makes your balances match the bank from day one.

## Add or rename accounts

**Settings → Accounts.** Names must be unique in your organization; capital letters and extra spaces don't count as different.

## Archive or delete

- An account that has **never been used** can be **deleted**.
- An account with **any activity** can only be **archived**. It disappears from pickers but its history stays in reports.

## Accounts and statement checks

Bank, cash and credit card accounts should be checked against statements every month. See [Statement checks](/ledger/everyday/statement-checks/).
