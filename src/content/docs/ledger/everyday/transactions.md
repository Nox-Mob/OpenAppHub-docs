## Record a transaction

**Transactions → New.** Enter:

- the **date**
- a **description** (and an optional note)
- the **accounts** and **amounts**: where the money came from and where it went
- optionally a **fund**, **project**, **category** or **tags**

Then **post** it.

### It must balance

Every transaction must balance: money out of one place equals money into another. In accounting terms, debits equal credits, with at least one of each. If it doesn't balance, you'll see a plain message and **nothing is recorded**.

Amounts can have at most two decimal places; more is refused, not rounded.

## Fixing a mistake: void, then re-enter

Posted transactions are **never edited**. To fix one:

1. Open it and **void** it. It stays in the history, marked void, and stops counting in balances and reports.
2. Enter the correct transaction.

Voiding can't be undone. This keeps a complete trail of what happened, which your auditor or board will appreciate.

## Closed periods

You can't post or void in a month or year that's been closed. See [Closing the books](/ledger/reports/closing/).

## Search and filter

**Transactions** can be filtered by:

- description or note
- dates
- account, project or fund
- amount range
- status (posted or void)
- source (entered by hand, imported, from a template)
- whether it's been checked against a statement

Filters stay in the page address, so you can bookmark or share a filtered view. Export the results to CSV.
