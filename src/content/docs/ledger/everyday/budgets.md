**Budgets** compares what you planned with what happened.

## Set a budget

1. **Budgets → Budget for:** choose the **Whole organization**, one **fund**, or one **project**.
2. Enter a yearly or monthly amount for each income and expense account.

## Budget vs actual

For the scope you chose, each account shows **Budget**, **Actual** and **Difference**, with a **Total**. A fund or project budget only counts transactions for that fund or project.

## Where budgets appear

- the **Budgets** page
- **Projects** (budget vs actual per project)
- the [board package](/ledger/reports/board-package/)

## Tip

Set next year's budget after closing the year, so you can see this year's actuals while planning.
