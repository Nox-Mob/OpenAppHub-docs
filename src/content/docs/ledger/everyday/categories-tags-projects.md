Accounts say **what** money was for. Categories, tags and projects add other ways to slice it.

| Use | When |
|---|---|
| **Category** | One extra grouping for each transaction, such as "Programs", "Admin", "Fundraising" |
| **Tags** | Any number of labels, such as "Spring gala" or "Volunteer" |
| **Project** | A piece of work with its own budget, such as "Roof repair". **Projects** shows budget vs actual spending for each. |

Add them when entering a transaction, or in Settings. Names must be unique in your organization (capital letters and extra spaces don't count).

## Archive and restore

Archive a category, tag or project you no longer use; it disappears from pickers but stays in past records. Typing an archived name offers to **restore** it instead of making a duplicate.

## Project budgets

Set a budget for a project and compare it to actual spending. See [Budgets](/ledger/everyday/budgets/).
