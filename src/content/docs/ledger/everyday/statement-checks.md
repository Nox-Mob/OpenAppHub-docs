A **statement check** confirms that your books match the bank. It's called a reconciliation in accounting.

## Start a check

**Statement check → Start a new statement.**

1. Choose the **account**.
2. Enter the statement's dates (**Statement from** and **Statement to**) and the **Ending balance (from statement)**. The beginning balance comes from your last check.
3. Choose **How do you want to check?**
   - **Simple:** the app matches imported bank rows to your books automatically, and you review what's left.
   - **Full:** tick each item yourself, like balancing a checkbook.

## Finish

Tick (or confirm) every item that appears on the statement. The difference must reach **zero** before you can finish. If it doesn't:

- look for transactions you haven't recorded (bank fees, interest),
- look for amounts entered wrongly,
- check the dates: items near the end of the month may belong to next month.

When you finish, the checked items are locked, so a later change can't quietly break a statement that already matched.

## Reopening a check

Admins and treasurers can reopen a finished check if it needs correcting. Discarding an unfinished check needs permission to edit the books.

## The report

The **statement check report** lists matched and outstanding items and the difference. Find it under Reports or **History** on the statement check page.

## Why it matters at month end

When you [close a month](/ledger/reports/closing/), the app warns you about bank, cash and card accounts not yet checked through month end.
