Nonprofits often receive money that can only be used for a purpose a donor chose, such as "for the youth program". **Funds** track that money separately.

## Kinds of fund

| Kind | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Unrestricted** | Your organization decides how to use it |
| **Restricted by donor** | Must be used for the donor's purpose |

## Create a fund

**Funds**: add a fund with its name and whether it's restricted. Names must be unique.

A fund's restriction **locks once it has transactions**, so restricted money can't quietly become unrestricted.

## Using funds

When you record a gift or an expense, choose the **fund** it belongs to. Transactions with no fund are unrestricted.

## Releasing restricted money

When you spend restricted money on its purpose, or the restriction is met, record a **release from restriction**. The money moves from "with donor restrictions" to "without donor restrictions" in your net assets.

## Fund balances

**Funds** shows each fund's **Received**, **Spent**, **Released** and **Remaining**.

The **fund activity report** (Money by Purpose) shows opening balance, received, spent, released and closing balance for each fund, and it's included in the [board package](/ledger/reports/board-package/).

## Archiving a fund

Archive funds you no longer use. A restricted fund that **still holds money can't be archived**; use or release the money first.

## Fund budgets

Set a budget for one fund on the [Budgets](/ledger/everyday/budgets/) page.
