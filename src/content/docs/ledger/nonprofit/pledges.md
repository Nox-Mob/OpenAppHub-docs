A **pledge** is a gift a donor has promised but not yet fully paid.

## Record a pledge

**Pledges → Record a pledge.** Enter the **donor**, the **amount** promised and the **fund** (or **No fund (unrestricted)**).

## Record payments

When money arrives, record a payment against the pledge. The list shows **Promised**, **Paid** and **Still owed**, with a **status**.

## Write off

If part of a pledge won't arrive, write it off. The pledge closes and your books show the reduced amount.

## Reports

Pledges feed into your fund balances and reports, so promised money for a restricted purpose appears in the right fund.
