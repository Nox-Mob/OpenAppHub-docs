A **pledge** is a gift someone has promised but not yet fully paid, such as _$1,200 over the year_. Nonprofit accounting counts it as income when it's promised, and tracks what's still owed until it's paid.

## Recording a pledge

Select **Pledges**, then **Record a pledge**:

| Field          |                                                                                                                     |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Donor**      | Who promised it                                                                                                     |
| **Amount**     | The total promised                                                                                                  |
| **Fund**       | A restricted fund, or **No fund (unrestricted)** ([Funds](/ledger/nonprofit/funds-and-budgets/#funds))              |
| Income account | The income account it counts towards. If there isn't one, OpenLedgerApp asks you to turn on an income account first |

Recording it books the promise: income, and an amount owed to you.

## Recording payments

When money arrives, find the pledge and choose **Record a payment**: the amount and date, and the bank account it went into. The list shows **Promised**, **Paid** and **Still owed** for each pledge. When the full amount has been paid its **Status** becomes **Paid**.

## Writing off what won't come

If a donor can't complete a pledge, choose **Write off what is left**. The remaining amount is removed from what you're owed and from income, and the status becomes **Written off**. Payments already received stay.

## Statuses

| Status          |                         |
| --------------- | ----------------------- |
| **Open**        | Something is still owed |
| **Paid**        | Fully paid              |
| **Written off** | The rest won't be paid  |

Owners, admins and treasurers can record pledges and payments.
