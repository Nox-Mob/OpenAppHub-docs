---
title: "Funds, projects, budgets and pledges"
description: "Track money by purpose."
sidebar:
  order: 1
---

- **Budgets**: plan spending and see how you're doing against it.
- **Projects** and **Funds**: tag money for a project or a fund (for example a restricted grant).
- **Pledges**: record money promised to you.

:::note
Funds are tags, not full fund accounting.
:::
