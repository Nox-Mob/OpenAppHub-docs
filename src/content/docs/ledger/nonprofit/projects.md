A **project** is a piece of work with its own budget: a renovation, a summer program, an event. Tag transactions with the project and OpenLedgerApp shows how much of the budget is spent.

## Adding a project

On **Projects**, type the **Project name** (_Summer Food Program_) and its **Budget**, then add it. Owners, admins and treasurers can add projects.

## Tagging transactions

When entering a transaction, choose the project. In **Advanced entry**, each line can have its own project, so one bill can be split between projects.

## Budget against actual

For each project the list shows **Budget**, **Spent** and **Remaining**, and its **Status**. Spent counts expenses tagged with the project.

**Archive** a finished project to tidy the list; **Restore** brings it back. Its transactions and reports are kept either way.

Projects are different from [funds](/ledger/nonprofit/funds-and-budgets/#funds): a fund tracks money given for a purpose (restricted), a project tracks spending on a piece of work. A project can be paid for from a fund.
