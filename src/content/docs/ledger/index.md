OpenLedgerApp is approachable double-entry accounting for small nonprofits and organizations.

:::caution[Pre-release software]
OpenLedgerApp is pre-1.0. Review every result and keep independent backups before relying on it for real books.
It organizes records and produces reports; it doesn't give financial, tax or legal advice.
:::
