**Double-entry**: every transaction is recorded in at least two accounts, and the amounts balance.

**Debit** and **credit**: the two sides of a transaction line.

**Reconcile**: check your books against the bank statement.

**Void**: cancel a posted transaction while keeping it in the history.

**Close the books**: lock a finished period so it can't be changed.
