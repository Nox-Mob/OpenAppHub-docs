OpenLedgerApp is open source and runs on its own, with its own sign-in, on your own Supabase database (supabase.com or
self-hosted with Docker). The full guide is in the repository's README:
[github.com/Nox-Mob/OpenLedgerApp](https://github.com/Nox-Mob/OpenLedgerApp).
