## Getting started

**Do I need to know accounting?**
No. Choose the **Simplest** wording and the app talks about "money in" and "money out". The double-entry rules are handled for you.

**Is it ready for our real books?**
OpenLedgerApp is pre-release (0.1.1). Try it with sample data first, keep backups, and keep your accountant involved.

**Is this tax or accounting advice?**
No. The app keeps records; it doesn't give advice. Check how to treat anything unusual with your accountant.

## Everyday use

**I made a mistake. How do I edit a transaction?**
You don't edit; you **void** it and enter it again. See [Recording transactions](/ledger/everyday/transactions/).

**Why does my bank import not show up in my balances?**
Imported rows are evidence only. Post each row to add it to your books.

**Can it connect to my bank automatically?**
Not yet. Download a file from your bank and import it.

**Can I attach receipts to transactions?**
Not yet.

**Can I send invoices or track bills?**
Not yet. OpenLedgerApp records money when it moves (cash basis). Invoices, bills, and money owed to and by you are planned.

**Does it do payroll?**
No.

**Can I use more than one currency?**
One currency per organization.

## Nonprofits

**Does it handle restricted gifts?**
Yes, with [funds](/ledger/nonprofit/funds/), releases from restriction and the fund activity report.

**Can it track pledges?**
Yes. See [Pledges](/ledger/nonprofit/pledges/).

## Editions

**Can several people use the desktop app?**
No. It's for one person. Use the web app for a team.

**Can the desktop app sync with the web app?**
Not yet.

## Data

**How do I back up my books?**
Settings → Exports → **Full backup** (web). See [Backups and exports](/ledger/admin/backups-and-exports/).

**Will restoring a backup overwrite my books?**
No. A restore always creates a new organization.

**Who can see our books?**
Only people you invite, with the role you give them.
