**Can I edit a posted transaction?**
No. Void it and enter it again; the history stays intact.

**Does importing a bank file change my books?**
No. Imported lines are evidence until you post them.

**Is it ready for real books?**
It's pre-1.0. Review every result and keep independent backups.
