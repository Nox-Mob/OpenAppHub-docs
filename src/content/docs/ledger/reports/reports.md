**Reports.** Names below are the **Simple** wording, with the accounting term in brackets. Your wording setting decides what you see, and you can rename any report in Settings.

| Report | What it answers |
|---|---|
| **Income Statement** (Profit & Loss; Statement of Activities for nonprofits) | What came in and went out over a period, and the bottom line |
| **Accounts Summary** (Balance Sheet; Statement of Financial Position) | What you have and owe on a date |
| **Account Totals** (Trial Balance) | Every account's total, which must balance |
| **Account History** (General Ledger) | Every transaction in every account, with opening, running and closing balances |
| **Account activity** | One account's transactions with running balance |
| **Fund Activity** (nonprofits) | Each fund's opening, received, spent, released and closing |
| **Statement Check** (Reconciliation Report) | Matched and outstanding items for a statement |
| **Budget vs actual** | Planned against actual, for the whole organization, a fund or a project |

Every report shows its **period** and **Cash basis**: income and expenses are counted when money moves.

## Drill down

On the income statement, balance sheet or trial balance, click any account to see the transactions behind its number.

## Export

- Each report exports to **CSV**, **Excel** and **PDF**.
- **Export all (Excel)** saves every report in one workbook, one sheet each.
- **Export all (PDF)** saves every report in one PDF, one page each.

Excel exports use Accounting format: currency symbol on the left, thousands separators, negatives in brackets.
