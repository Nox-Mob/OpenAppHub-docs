The **board package** puts the reports a board usually asks for into one PDF:

1. a cover page
2. the income statement
3. the balance sheet
4. budget vs actual
5. fund activity (nonprofits)

## Create it

Choose the period (usually last month or year to date) and generate the board package. It works in the web app and the desktop app.

## Bring it to the meeting

Send the PDF to your board, or add it to the meeting's documents in [OpenBoardApp](/board/records/documents-and-packets/) so it's part of the board packet.

## Before you send it

Run your [statement checks](/ledger/everyday/statement-checks/) for the period first, so the numbers match the bank.
