title: "Closing the books"
description: "Close a month, close a fiscal year, lock the books, and reopen with a reason."

---

Closing a period stops anyone changing it by accident. **Settings → Close the books.** Admins and treasurers can close and reopen.

## Close a month

1. **Close a month** → choose the month.
2. The app warns about:
   - bank, cash and credit card accounts **not checked against a statement** through month end,
   - **unfinished** statement checks.
3. Fix them, or close anyway. Closing with warnings asks you to confirm, and the warnings are saved in the history.

## Close a fiscal year

**Close a fiscal year** → choose the year. The year's income and expenses are closed out, and the books are locked through year end, in one step. If anything fails, nothing is kept. **Closed years** lists them.

## Lock the books

**Lock the books** up to a date without a full close, for example after sending reports to your accountant. Nothing on or before that date can be posted or voided.

## Reopen

Need to fix something in a closed period? **Reopen books**, with a **written reason** (at least 10 characters). The reason, who did it and which closed years were reopened are saved in the history.

After the correction, close the period again.

## Two people at once

If two people close (or post into) a period at the same moment, the app records exactly one result. A transaction can't sneak into a period that's being closed.
