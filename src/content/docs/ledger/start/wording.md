OpenLedgerApp always keeps proper double-entry books underneath. The **wording** setting only changes the words on screen and in reports, so people who aren't accountants can use it comfortably.

Change it any time: **Settings**, wording. You can pick a level for everything, or a different word for any single term.

## What changes

Words for a business (nonprofit wording in brackets where it differs):

| Term | Simplest | Simple | Double-entry |
|---|---|---|---|
| What you own | Money You Have | Accounts | Assets |
| What you owe | Money You Owe | Debts | Liabilities |
| What's left over | What's Yours (Net Assets) | Owner's Equity (Net Assets) | Equity (Net Assets) |
| Money coming in | Money In | Income | Revenue (Revenue & Support) |
| Money going out | Money Out | Expenses | Expenses |
| Entry sides | Increase / Decrease | Increase / Decrease | Debit / Credit |
| Bottom line | Profit (Net Change) | Net Income (Net Change) | Net Income (Change in Net Assets) |
| Advanced entry | Transaction | Transaction | Journal Entry |

## Report names

| Report | Simplest | Simple | Double-entry |
|---|---|---|---|
| Income report | Income Statement (Money In & Out) | Income Statement (Income & Expenses) | Profit & Loss (Statement of Activities) |
| Position report | What You Have & Owe | Accounts Summary | Balance Sheet (Statement of Financial Position) |
| Totals check | Totals Check | Account Totals | Trial Balance |
| Every transaction | Every Transaction | Account History | General Ledger |
| Fund report | Money by Purpose | Fund Activity | Fund Activity |
| Bank statement report | Bank Statement Check | Statement Check | Reconciliation Report |

These docs use the **Simple** wording, with the accounting term where it helps.
