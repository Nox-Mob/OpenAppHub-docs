OpenLedgerApp is the same bookkeeping app in three forms.

| | Desktop app | Web, self-hosted | Web, hosted |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Best for** | One person keeping one small organization's books | Teams with someone technical | Teams who want it to just work |
| **People** | One (you) | Several, with roles | Several, with roles |
| **Sign-in** | None | Email, Google, two-step | Email, Google, two-step |
| **Where books live** | A file on your computer | Your Supabase database | Our servers |
| **Internet** | Not needed | Needed | Needed |
| **Cost** | Free | Free software, your own hosting | Subscription (coming soon) |
| **Status** | Preview | Available | Coming soon |

## The desktop app

Free, for Windows, macOS and Linux. Your books stay on your computer and work offline. It's a **preview**: the core books work, but some features are still web-only. See [The desktop app](/ledger/start/desktop-app/).

## The web app

Use the web app when more than one person needs the books, for example a treasurer, a bookkeeper and a board member who only reads reports. Each person signs in with their own account and [role](/ledger/admin/members-and-roles/).

## Moving between editions

Desktop and web can't sync with each other yet. Choose the one that fits how your organization works today.
