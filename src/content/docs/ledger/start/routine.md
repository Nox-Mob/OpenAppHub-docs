## Every week

- Record what came in and went out, or [import your bank file](/ledger/everyday/importing/) and post the rows.
- Post anything waiting in **Ready to review** from your [recurring templates](/ledger/everyday/templates/).
- Record [pledge payments](/ledger/nonprofit/pledges/) received (nonprofits).

## Every month

1. Run a [statement check](/ledger/everyday/statement-checks/) for each bank and credit card account, against the month's statement.
2. Look at [budget vs actual](/ledger/everyday/budgets/).
3. Create the [board package](/ledger/reports/board-package/) for your board meeting.
4. [Close the month](/ledger/reports/closing/), so nobody can change it by accident.

## Every year

1. Make sure every month is checked and closed.
2. Run the year's reports and send them to your accountant or board.
3. [Close the fiscal year](/ledger/reports/closing/).
4. Download a [full backup](/ledger/admin/backups-and-exports/) and keep it somewhere safe.
5. Set next year's budgets.

The built-in **tutorial** (Settings → Your preferences) walks through this routine.
