The desktop app keeps your books in a file on your own computer. There's no account, no sign-in and no internet needed. It's free and meant for one person keeping one organization's books.

:::caution[Preview]
The desktop app is a preview. The core books work, but some features are still web-only (listed below). Keep your own copies of important reports.
:::

## Installing

Installers for **Windows**, **macOS** (Apple Silicon and Intel) and **Linux** are published with each desktop release on the [OpenLedgerApp GitHub releases page](https://github.com/Nox-Mob/OpenLedgerApp/releases). If no desktop installer is listed there yet, the preview hasn't been published.

On macOS the app isn't signed yet, so macOS may warn you the first time you open it. Open it from Finder with right-click → **Open**.

## First launch

The app opens straight into organization setup (no website, no sign-in). After that, it opens the ledger for the organization you used last.

## What works in the desktop app

- Organizations and settings, accounts and opening balances
- Transactions: post, void, search and filter
- Templates and recurring drafts
- Statement checks
- Budgets for the whole organization, funds and projects
- Funds, releases, pledges and pledge payments, projects, categories and tags, with archive and restore
- All reports and the board package
- Locking the books, month and year close, and reopening
- The first-run checklist, tutorial and diagnostics

## Not in the desktop app yet

These show "isn't available in the desktop app yet. Nothing was recorded." Use the web app if you need them:

- members and invitations (the desktop app is single-user)
- bank file import and PDF statement reading
- full backups and restore
- exports to CSV, Excel and PDF
- the history (audit) viewer

## Where your data is

Your books are in a database file in the app's data folder on your computer. Back up your computer regularly; until in-app backups arrive on desktop, that's how your books are protected.

## Desktop and web together

The desktop app and the web app don't sync yet.
