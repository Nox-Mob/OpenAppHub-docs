OpenLedgerApp keeps your organization's books. It's made for small nonprofits and associations whose treasurer may be a volunteer, not an accountant. It records the money that comes in and goes out, keeps track of which money is for what, and makes the reports your board needs.

## The menu

The menu on the left has these parts:

| Menu                                              | What it's for                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Dashboard**                                     | The big picture: how much cash you have, money in and out this month, and anything that needs your attention. Start here.                 |
| **Transactions**                                  | Every bit of money in or out, one line each. Select **New Transaction** to add one, like a donation or a bill you paid.                   |
| **Templates**                                     | Transactions you enter again and again, like rent. Save one once, then reuse it with one click. They can also repeat on a schedule.       |
| **Accounts**                                      | Your _chart of accounts_: the list of "buckets" your money is sorted into, like _Checking_, _Membership dues_ or _Rent_.                  |
| **Import Bank File**                              | Bring in a file of transactions from your bank, so you don't type them by hand. You check each one before it goes in.                     |
| **Reconcile**                                     | Compare your books to your bank statement, line by line, to make sure they match.                                                         |
| **Projects**, **Budgets**, **Funds**, **Pledges** | Track money by what it's for: a project like a summer festival, a yearly budget, money given for a special purpose, and promises to give. |
| **Reports**                                       | Standard reports, like what you have (balance sheet) and what came in and went out (income statement).                                    |
| **Settings**                                      | People and their roles, two-step sign-in, closing the books for a month, exports and more.                                                |

## Where to start

1. **Set up your accounts.** The first time you open OpenLedgerApp, a guided setup suggests a list of accounts for an organization like yours. You can change it any time. See [Accounts](/ledger/everyday/accounts/).
2. **Enter your starting balances.** Tell OpenLedgerApp how much is in each bank account on the day you start.
3. **Add transactions as they happen**, or import them from your bank.
4. **Check against your bank each month** with **Reconcile**.
5. **Close the month** so nobody changes it by accident, and share the reports with your board.

If your organization also uses OpenCharterApp, membership dues can come into your books for you. See [Dues from OpenCharterApp](/ledger/everyday/charter-payments/).
