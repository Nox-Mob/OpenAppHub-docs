The first time you sign in (or open the desktop app), setup takes three steps.

## 1. Organization

- **Name.**
- **Type:** **Business** or **Nonprofit**. Nonprofits get funds, pledges, nonprofit report names and "net assets" wording.
- **Currency** (for example USD). One currency per organization.
- **Fiscal year:** the month your year starts.

## 2. Accounts

Tick the accounts you need from the suggested list: bank and cash accounts, credit cards, income and expense accounts. You can add, rename and archive accounts later. See [Accounts](/ledger/everyday/accounts/).

## 3. Display

Choose the wording the app uses. See [Plain wording or accounting terms](/ledger/start/wording/).

| Level | Example words |
|---|---|
| **Simplest** | "Money you have", "Money in / out" |
| **Simple** | "Accounts", "Income", "Expenses" |
| **Double-entry** | "Assets", "Revenue", "Debits / Credits" |

## After setup

- The **dashboard** shows a **first-run checklist** that ticks itself off as you go: opening balances, first transaction, first statement check and so on. Hide it when you're done.
- Turn on the **tutorial** (Settings → **Your preferences**) for a guided tour of getting started and the weekly, monthly and yearly routine.

## Next steps

2. [Record transactions](/ledger/everyday/transactions/) or [import a bank file](/ledger/everyday/importing/).
