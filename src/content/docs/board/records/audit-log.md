title: "Audit log"
description: "Who changed what and when."
sidebar:
  order: 4
---

**Audit** lists every change to your organization's records: **When**, **Action** and **Record**, with who made it.

- Every change is recorded at the same moment it's saved.
- Nobody can edit or delete audit entries.
- Owners, administrators, chairs and secretaries can read it.

Use it to answer questions like "who changed the agenda?" or "when was this document replaced?".
