Open a meeting → **Minutes**.

## The minutes workflow

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Being written. Edit freely. |
| **Submitted** | Ready for approval (**Submit for approval**) |
| **Approved** | The official minutes (**Approve as official minutes**). Locked. |
| **Superseded** | Replaced by an amended version. Locked and kept. |

Chairs, secretaries, administrators and owners can approve minutes. Usually the board approves the previous meeting's minutes at its next meeting, and the secretary then presses **Approve as official minutes**.

The **Dashboard** lists minutes awaiting approval.

## Writing minutes

The minutes are assembled from the meeting's records: attendance, agenda notes and motions with their vote results. Edit the text as needed.

## Amending approved minutes

Approved minutes can't be edited. If the board amends them, create a **new version**: it goes through approval, and once approved the old version becomes **Superseded**. **Versions** shows every version.

## Print or save as PDF

Download the minutes as a formatted PDF on your [letterhead](/board/records/letterhead/). Titles shown are the ones people held on the meeting date.

## What gets locked

When minutes are approved, the meeting's minutes, attendance and motions become part of the official record and can't be changed.
