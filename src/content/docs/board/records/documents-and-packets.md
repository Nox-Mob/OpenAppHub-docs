## The document library

**Documents → Add a document**: give it a **title**, a **category**, the **file**, and optionally the **meeting** it belongs to (or **Not tied to a meeting**). Filter by category with **All categories**.

Uploading a new file to a document keeps the earlier versions.

Files are private to your organization. Download links work for about a minute, so a copied link can't be passed around.

## Board packets

A **board packet** is one PDF with everything the board needs before a meeting.

1. Open a meeting → **Board packet**.
2. Choose documents from the library (**This meeting**, or others).
3. **Assemble packet.** The app builds one PDF, with generated pages on your letterhead.

Attached PDFs are added as they are; your letterhead isn't painted over them.

## Packet versions and distribution

Each time you assemble, a new entry appears under **Packet versions**. A packet is **Not distributed** until you mark it distributed. A distributed packet is locked, so everyone can trust they're looking at the same version.

Members can open the packet from the live meeting dashboard during the meeting.

## OpenLedgerApp's board package

The treasurer can create a financial **board package** PDF in OpenLedgerApp and add it to the library for the meeting.
