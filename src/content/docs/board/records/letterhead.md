**Settings → Organization letterhead** (owners and administrators).

## Upload your stationery

Upload **Your stationery** as a **PNG**, **JPG** or **PDF**. It's used as the background for pages the app generates: minutes, and the generated pages of board packets.

## Leave room for the header and footer

Set **Header space (points)** and **Footer space (points)** so text doesn't print over your logo or address. 72 points is one inch.

The page explains how to prepare each format (**PDF preparation**, **PNG / JPG preparation**):

- **PDF** is best: export a letter-size (or A4) page from your design tool with the logo and footer in place.
- **PNG / JPG:** use a full page at high resolution so it prints sharply.

## Good to know

- Only owners and administrators can change the letterhead.
- Documents you attach to packets keep their own look; letterhead is only added to generated pages.
- Minutes you generate later use the current letterhead. Packets you already saved keep the letterhead they were made with.
- If you see **Letterhead could not be loaded**, upload it again.
