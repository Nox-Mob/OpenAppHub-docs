## Minutes

On a meeting's **Minutes** tab, generate a draft from the meeting (attendance, motions and results, notes against each agenda item), then edit it.

| Status        | Meaning                                                                                   | Who                              |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| **Draft**     | Being written. **Save draft** as often as you like                                        | Owners, admins, chair, secretary |
| **Submitted** | Ready for the board to review (**Submit for approval**). **Return to draft** to change it | Same                             |
| **Approved**  | **Approve as official minutes**: the official record. Who approved and when is recorded   | Owners, admins, chair, secretary |

Approved minutes are locked: they, and the meeting's motions and attendance, can't be changed or deleted. To correct approved minutes, add an **amended version** and approve it; the earlier version is marked **superseded** and stays readable.

Minutes print on your organization's **letterhead** when you set one up.

## Letterhead

Under **Settings & export**, upload your letterhead (a PDF, PNG or JPG of your header and footer: **Upload letterhead**). **Header space** and **Footer space** (in points; 72 points is one inch) set how much room it takes at the top and bottom of each page, so text doesn't print over it. It's used for minutes and board packets.

## Documents

**Documents** keeps your bylaws, policies, reports and anything else the board needs:

- Add a document with a **Title**, a **Category**, an optional **Meeting** it belongs to, and a **File**. The document and its first file are saved together.
- Upload a new **version** of a document any time; every version is kept, and the latest is used in board packets.
- Download links are private and expire after a minute, so sharing a link outside the board doesn't give lasting access.

## The audit log and exports

More detail: [The audit log](/board/records/audit-log/) and [Settings and export](/board/records/settings-and-export/).

Every change to the board's records is logged (who, what, when). Owners, admins, the chair and the secretary can read the audit log.

Under **Settings & export**, owners and admins can export the **full board record** for a date range (**From**, **To**) and everything else as spreadsheets. Exports, reading and the audit log keep working even when your plan makes the app read-only.

## Reports from the other apps

When OpenCharterApp or OpenLedgerApp sends a report to a board package, it appears in **Documents** under
**Report from OpenCharterApp** or **Report from OpenLedgerApp**, attached to that meeting, and goes into its board packet.
