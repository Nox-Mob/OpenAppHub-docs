- **Minutes** are generated on your organization's letterhead, approved, then locked as the official version.
- **Documents** keeps bylaws, policies and reports with versions and private download links.
- Every change is logged, and you can export the full board record at any time.

## Reports from the other apps

When OpenCharterApp or OpenLedgerApp sends a report to a board package, it appears in **Documents** under
**Report from OpenCharterApp** or **Report from OpenLedgerApp**, attached to that meeting, and goes into its board packet.
