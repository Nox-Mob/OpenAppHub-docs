**Settings & export** in the side menu. Owners and administrators change settings and run exports.

## Organization

| Setting       |                                                     |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **Name**      | Your organization's name, as shown in OpenBoardApp  |
| **Time zone** | Used for meeting times, reminders and the audit log |

Hosted on OpenAppHub, the name and time zone are your organization's own, shared with every app (change them in OpenAppHub's **Organization settings**).

## Voting

| Setting                                     |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Quorum (members required)**               | How many board members must be present for the meeting to make decisions                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **How votes are recorded**                  | **Vote totals only** (roll call optional per motion), or **Record how each board member voted**                                                                                                                                            |
| **Decision rule for live votes**            | What a motion needs to pass, such as a simple majority of those voting                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **How live votes close**                    | One or more of: the chair (or another permitted role) closes it; it closes automatically once every eligible member has voted; it closes when a timer runs out (**Timer (minutes)**). A vote closes as soon as any chosen condition is met |
| **Who can close a vote and record results** | Which roles may close a live vote                                                                                                                                                                                                          |

Each is explained in [Motions and votes](/board/voting/motions-and-votes/#voting-settings).

## Letterhead

Upload your header and footer (a PDF, PNG or JPG) and minutes, agendas and packets print on it. See [Minutes, documents and export](/board/records/minutes-and-documents/#letterhead).

## Data export

**Download full export (JSON)** downloads the full board record in one file: the organization and its settings, letterhead details, people, offices, positions and terms, committees and their members, meetings, agenda items, attendance, motions and votes, minutes, documents and their versions, packets, action items, meeting templates, and the audit log. (Files themselves, such as uploaded documents, are listed by name; download them from **Documents**.) Use it to keep your own copy or to move to another system. It works even when your plan makes OpenBoardApp read-only.

## Another organization

Self-hosted, you can create another organization here. Hosted, organizations are created in OpenAppHub.
