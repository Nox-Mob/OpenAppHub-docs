OpenBoardApp helps a small nonprofit's board run its meetings and keep its official records.

## What it does

- **People:** the board roster, board roles with term lengths and history, committees, and invitations.
- **Meetings:** schedule meetings from templates, build agendas, take attendance and notes.
- **Live voting:** board members vote from their own devices during the meeting. The app checks quorum, applies your decision rule and saves an official vote record that can't be changed.
- **Minutes:** draft, approve and lock minutes; amendments create a new version. Print them as a PDF on your letterhead.
- **Documents:** a private document library and board packets assembled into one PDF.
- **Action items:** tasks from meetings with owners and due dates.
- **Records:** an audit log of every change, and exports including a full board-record ZIP.
