| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **App role** | What someone can do in the app: Owner, Administrator, Chair, Secretary, Treasurer, Board member or Observer |
| **Board role / title** | An office such as Chair or Treasurer, with a term length and holder history. Doesn't change app access |
| **Term** | A period someone holds a board role |
| **Committee** | A group of the board with a purpose and members |
| **Template** | A reusable meeting outline with a standard agenda |
| **Agenda item** | A topic on the agenda; may be a vote item |
| **Vote item** | An agenda item that needs a decision |
| **Motion** | A proposal the board decides on |
| **Live vote** | A vote cast from members' own devices during an in-progress meeting |
| **Eligible voters (electorate)** | Members present or remote with a linked account when the vote opens; fixed for that vote |
| **Quorum** | The number of members who must be present for a decision |
| **Decision rule** | How the result is worked out: majority of votes cast, majority of those present, two-thirds, or chair-recorded |
| **Abstain** | Take part without voting yes or no |
| **Recuse** | Step out of a decision entirely |
| **Disposition** | The result an authorized person records, with an explanation, when the app can't decide by itself |
| **Carried / Failed / Tabled / Withdrawn** | Motion outcomes |
| **Official vote record** | The permanent, unchangeable record saved when a vote closes |
| **Minutes** | The official record of a meeting: draft, submitted, approved, superseded |
| **Superseded** | Replaced by an amended version, kept for the record |
| **Board packet** | One PDF with the documents for a meeting |
| **Distributed** | A packet sent to the board; locked from then on |
| **Letterhead** | Your stationery, used on generated pages |
| **Action item** | A follow-up task with an owner and due date |
| **Audit log** | The permanent record of every change |
| **Board record ZIP** | An export of minutes, votes, attendance and packets, one folder per meeting |
