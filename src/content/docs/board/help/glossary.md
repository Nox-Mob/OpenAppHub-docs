**Quorum**: the minimum number of members present for the meeting to make decisions.

**Motion**: a proposal put to a vote.

**Recusal**: a member stepping out of a vote, for example because of a conflict of interest.

**Minutes**: the official written record of a meeting.

**Board packet**: the agenda and documents members get before a meeting.
