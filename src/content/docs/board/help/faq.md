## Voting

**Can someone change their vote?**
No. Each person confirms their vote as official, and it can't be changed. If the board wants to revisit a decision, make a new motion.

**Are votes secret?**
No. Secret ballots aren't supported. You can choose to record totals only instead of each person's vote.

**Can members vote by email between meetings?**
Not yet. Live votes happen during a meeting that's in progress.

**Someone joined late. Why can't they vote?**
Eligible voters are fixed when the vote opens. They can vote on the next motion.

**Someone is attending by phone. Can they vote?**
Yes, if they're marked **Remote** and have an account linked. Otherwise record their vote by roll call.

**What's the difference between abstaining and recusing?**
Abstaining means taking part without voting yes or no. Recusing means stepping out of the decision entirely, for example over a conflict of interest. See [Live voting](/board/voting/live-voting/).

## Minutes and records

**Can I fix a typo in approved minutes?**
Not in place. Create a new version, approve it, and the old version is kept as superseded.

**Can the app record the meeting or write minutes for us?**
No. Transcription and AI-written minutes aren't part of OpenBoardApp.

**Do you support e-signatures?**
Not yet.

## People

**Is a "Treasurer" title the same as the Treasurer app role?**
No. The title records the office; the app role controls access. See [Roles and permissions](/board/people/roles/).

**Do committee members need accounts?**
No. Committees list people by name.

**How do I hand the organization to the new chair?**
Settings → **Transfer ownership**.

## Other

**Does it send meeting reminders?**
Not yet; reminders are planned.

**Does it connect to my calendar or Zoom?**
No calendar or video integrations yet.

**Does it work offline?**
No. OpenBoardApp needs an internet connection.

**Does it handle accounting or dues?**
No. Use [OpenLedgerApp](/ledger/) for the books and [OpenCharterApp](/charter/) for members and dues.
