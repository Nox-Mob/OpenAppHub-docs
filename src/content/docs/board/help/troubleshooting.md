| Problem | Likely cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "Votes can only be opened while the meeting is in progress" | The meeting isn't **In progress** | Change the meeting's status to In progress |
| Can't open a second vote | Another vote is still open in this meeting | Close the open vote first |
| A member can't vote | Not marked present or remote, no linked account, or they arrived after the vote opened | Mark attendance and link their account before opening the next vote; record their vote by roll call if needed |
| "No account linked — can't vote live" | The attendee hasn't accepted an invitation or isn't linked | Invite them, or link their account on the attendance record |
| The vote result says it needs the chair | Quorum wasn't met, nobody voted yes or no, or the rule is custom | Use **Record the result** with an explanation |
| "Approved minutes are part of the official record" | Approved minutes are locked | Create a new version |
| Can't edit attendance or motions | The meeting's minutes are approved, or it's archived | These are locked; record corrections in amended minutes |
| Can't change a packet | It's been distributed | Assemble a new version |
| "Letterhead could not be loaded" | The file is missing or unreadable | Upload it again in Settings |
| Text prints over the letterhead | Header or footer space too small | Increase **Header space** or **Footer space** |
| An invitation link doesn't work | Used, expired (14 days) or revoked | Create a new invitation |
| Can't remove someone | They're the owner | Transfer ownership first |
| A document is missing from the board record ZIP | It couldn't be downloaded while building the ZIP | Download it from the library and add it to your copy |

Still stuck? See [Getting help](/hub/help/support/).
