Each meeting has tabs that follow the meeting's life:

1. **Agenda & notes**: build the agenda and take notes against each item.
2. **Attendance**: record who's present; quorum is shown live.
3. **Motions & votes**: record motions, movers, seconders, recusals and the vote.
4. **Minutes**: generate formal minutes, submit them for approval, and lock the official version.
5. **Board packet**: assemble the agenda and documents into one PDF for members.
6. **Action items**: assign follow-ups with due dates that stay visible after the meeting.

## Scheduling a meeting

On **Meetings**, select **New meeting** and fill in:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Start from** | A [meeting template](#meeting-templates), or the standard agenda (call to order, approval of previous minutes, treasurer's report, old business, new business, adjournment) |
| **Title**, **Date**, **Start time**, **Location** | The time is in your organization's time zone (**Settings & export**) |

The meeting and its starting agenda are saved together: you never get a meeting without its agenda. Change the agenda freely afterwards.

## Meeting templates

**Meeting templates** keep a reusable title, time, place and agenda outline for meetings you hold regularly. Write the agenda one item per line: `Title | Presenter | Minutes`, for example `Treasurer's report | Tess | 10`. Or open a meeting and select **Save as template**.

## Agenda items that need a vote

Mark an agenda item as a **vote item** to prepare a motion in advance (the **proposed motion**). It uses your organization's **decision rule** unless you choose a different one for that item ([Motions and votes](/board/voting/motions-and-votes/)).

## The board packet

On **Board packet**, choose the documents to include (the agenda is always first) and select **Generate packet PDF**. Each PDF you build is kept as a new version (*Packet v1*, *v2*…) with a list of what's in it. **Mark as distributed** records that you sent it to the board, by email or on paper; it doesn't send anything itself.

Reports from the other apps can go straight in: in OpenCharterApp (**Reports**) and OpenLedgerApp (reports), **Add to a board package** sends that report as a PDF to an upcoming meeting's documents, ready for its next packet.

## Action items

On a meeting's **Action items** tab (or **Action items** in the menu), add follow-ups with an owner and a due date. They stay visible after the meeting until they're **Done**, and appear on the assignee's [OpenAppHub dashboard](/hub/start/dashboard/) and in their [task summary](/hub/accounts/task-summary/).

## Reminders

OpenBoardApp emails reminders on its own:

- **Meeting reminders**: everyone with a current term gets an email 7 days and 1 day before a scheduled meeting.
- **Action item reminders**: whoever an action item is assigned to gets one 2 days before it's due, and once more when it becomes overdue.

Moving a meeting or a due date sends a fresh reminder. Owners and admins can change the days, or switch either one off, on the **Email** page (**Reminders for** your organization); see [Automations](/hub/automations/). Each person can turn reminders off for themselves on their OpenAppHub **Account** page.
