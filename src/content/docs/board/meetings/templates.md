**Meeting templates** keep what you'd otherwise retype for each regular meeting: its title, start time, place and agenda. Pick one when you schedule a meeting, and everything is filled in for you to adjust.

Owners, administrators, the chair and the secretary can create and change templates. Everyone on the board can see them.

## Creating a template

1. Select **Meeting templates** in the side menu, then **New template**.
2. Fill in:

| Field                        |                                                                                           |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Template name**            | How you'll find it, such as _Annual general meeting_ or _Monthly board meeting_           |
| **Meeting title**            | The title each meeting gets                                                               |
| **Start time**, **Location** | Optional; you can change them per meeting                                                 |
| **Agenda**                   | One item per line, as `Title \| Presenter \| Minutes`. Presenter and minutes are optional |

For example:

```
Call to order | Chair | 2
Approval of the previous minutes | Secretary | 5
Treasurer's report | Treasurer | 10
Old business | | 15
New business | | 20
Adjournment | Chair | 1
```

3. Select **Save**. The template shows its agenda and, when you've given minutes, about how long the meeting runs.

A new template starts with the standard agenda filled in, so you only change what's different.

## Using a template

When you schedule a meeting, choose the template in **Start from** (see [Scheduling a meeting](/board/meetings/running-a-meeting/#scheduling-a-meeting)). The title, time, place and agenda are copied into the meeting; changing the meeting later doesn't change the template, and changing the template doesn't change meetings already scheduled.

## Changing or deleting

**Edit** changes a template for future meetings. **Delete** removes it; meetings made from it are kept.
