A **template** is a reusable meeting outline: a title, start time, location and agenda.

## Create a template

**Templates → New template.** Enter the **template name**, **meeting title**, **start time**, **location** and the **agenda** (each item's title, presenter and minutes). Vote items and their decision rules are saved too.

Or open a meeting and choose **Save as template**.

## Use a template

**Meetings → New meeting → Start from** your template. The meeting gets a copy of the agenda to change as needed.

## Changing a template

Edit or delete templates on the Templates page. Changes only affect meetings created afterwards; existing meetings keep their own agenda.
