Action items are the board's follow-ups: _send the draft budget to the treasurer_, _book the hall for the AGM_. Each has a description, the person it's assigned to, an optional due date and a status. They stay visible after the meeting until they're done.

## Where they are

- **Action items** in the side menu: everything for your organization. **My tasks** shows yours; **All** shows everyone's.
- A meeting's **Action items** tab: the ones added during or for that meeting. In the full list, each says which meeting it came from, with a link.
- OpenAppHub's **dashboard**: yours, under **Your tasks**, with a **Done** button ([The dashboard](/hub/start/dashboard/)).

## Adding one

Owners, administrators, the chair and the secretary can add action items. At the top of the list (or on the meeting's tab):

1. **What needs to happen?**: what's to be done, specific enough to know when it's finished.
2. **Assign to…**: one of the board's people.
3. A due date (optional, but reminders only go out for items with one).
4. Select **Add**.

## Statuses

| Status          | Meaning                         |
| --------------- | ------------------------------- |
| **open**        | Not started                     |
| **in progress** | Being worked on                 |
| **done**        | Finished (shown struck through) |
| **cancelled**   | No longer needed                |

An item past its due date that's still open or in progress shows **Overdue**.

The person an item is assigned to can change its status. Owners, administrators, the chair and the secretary can change any item's status.

## Reminders

OpenBoardApp's ready-made **Action item reminders** email the person an item is assigned to:

- **2 days before** it's due (owners and admins can change the number of days), and
- **once more the day after** it's due, if it's still open. Items more than a week overdue are left alone.

Owners and admins change these under **Email**, **Reminders for** your organization, or on OpenAppHub's Automations page ([Ready-made reminders](/hub/automations/ready-made/)). Each person can turn reminders off for themselves on their account page.

The reminder says the item is waiting for them. If your organization allows **Mark action items done from email** ([Actions from email](/hub/automations/actions-from-email/)), it also has a **Mark it done** button: they sign in, confirm, and it's marked done.

Workflows can do more, such as a second nudge a week later or a weekly list to each person: see the _Nudge overdue action items_ template ([Templates](/hub/automations/templates/)) and [Examples](/hub/automations/examples/#board).

## Good practice

- One person per item. If two people share it, split it into two items.
- Start with a verb: _Send_, _Book_, _Draft_, _Call_.
- Review open items at the start of each meeting (a standing agenda item such as _Action items from last meeting_).
