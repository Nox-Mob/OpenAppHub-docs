**Action items** are follow-up tasks: "Treasurer to get three quotes for the roof", for example.

## Add an action item

Open a meeting → **Action items**, and add the task with **who** is responsible and a **due date**.

## Statuses

**Open**, **In progress**, **Done** or **Cancelled**. Update the status as work moves along.

## Your tasks

- **Tasks** (My tasks) lists everything assigned to you.
- The **Dashboard** shows your action items and flags **Overdue** ones.

## Reviewing at the next meeting

Add an agenda item such as "Review action items" to your template so open items are checked at every meeting.
