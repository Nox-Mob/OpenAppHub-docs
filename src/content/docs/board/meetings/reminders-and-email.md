The **Email** page in OpenBoardApp's side menu gathers everything about email.

## Reminders for your organization

Two ready-made reminders, both on until switched off:

- **Meeting reminders**: each current board member, before every scheduled meeting (7 and 1 days before by default).
- **Action item reminders**: the person an action item is assigned to, before it's due (2 days by default) and once the day after it's due.

Owners and admins change their days or switch them off here, or on OpenAppHub's Automations page. All the settings are in [Ready-made reminders](/hub/automations/ready-made/).

## Actions from email

Whether people may mark action items done straight from a reminder's **Mark it done** button. Off until an owner or admin allows it. Hosted, it's set for every app in OpenAppHub's **Organization settings**; self-hosted, here. See [Actions from email](/hub/automations/actions-from-email/).

## Your own choices

Each person chooses for themselves whether they get reminders, their task summary email, and their notifications:

- **Hosted**: on your OpenAppHub account page ([Your account](/hub/accounts/account-settings/), [Notifications](/hub/accounts/notifications/)). The Email page links there.
- **Self-hosted**: on this page, under **Notifications** and your email choices.

## The email server (self-hosted)

A self-hosted OpenBoardApp sends its own email through the server you set up here, with a test email to check it. See [Self-hosting](/board/help/self-hosting/).
