## Schedule a meeting

1. **Meetings → New meeting** (Schedule a meeting).
2. **Start from:** a blank meeting or a [template](/board/meetings/templates/), such as your standard agenda.
3. Enter the **title**, **date**, **start time** and **location**.
4. Save.

A meeting from a template copies the template's agenda. Later changes to the template don't change meetings already created.

## Meeting statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Being prepared |
| **Scheduled** | Set for a date |
| **In progress** | Happening now. Live votes can only open and be cast in this status. |
| **Completed** | Finished |
| **Archived** | Filed away |

## Inside a meeting

Each meeting has tabs:

- **Agenda & notes:** see [Agenda and notes](/board/meetings/agenda-and-notes/)
- **Attendance:** see [Attendance and quorum](/board/meetings/attendance/)
- **Motions & votes:** see [Live voting](/board/voting/live-voting/)
- **Minutes:** see [Minutes](/board/records/minutes/)
- **Board packet:** see [Documents and board packets](/board/records/documents-and-packets/)
- **Action items:** see [Action items](/board/meetings/action-items/)

## The live meeting dashboard

During the meeting, members open the **live meeting dashboard** to follow the agenda, see the board packet and vote. See [Live voting](/board/voting/live-voting/).

## Save as a template

Liked how a meeting's agenda turned out? **Save as template** to reuse it.
