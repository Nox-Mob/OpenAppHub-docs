Open a meeting → **Attendance**.

## Mark attendance

For each board member, choose:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Present** | In the room |
| **Remote** | Attending by phone or video |
| **Excused** | Absent with notice |
| **Absent** | Absent |

**Add a guest by name** for non-voting attendees (staff, presenters, members of the public). Guests are marked **Guest / non-voting**.

## Who can vote live

To vote from their own device, a person must be **present or remote** and have an **account linked** to their attendance record. Someone shown as **No account linked — can't vote live** can't vote in the app; record their vote by roll call instead, or link their account.

Being signed in to the app doesn't count as attending. Attendance is what decides who can vote.

## Quorum

The **live quorum indicator** compares attendance to the quorum in **Settings** (the number of members required). If quorum isn't met, a live vote can't produce an automatic result; the chair records the outcome with an explanation. See [The official vote record](/board/voting/official-record/).

## Locked after approval

Attendance for a meeting whose minutes are approved, or that's archived, is locked.
