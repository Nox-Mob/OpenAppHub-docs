Open a meeting → **Agenda & notes**.

## Build the agenda

**Add item** for each topic, with a title, presenter and time (minutes) if you like.

For items that need a decision, answer **Is this a vote item?** with yes. You can also give a vote item its own **decision rule** (for example two-thirds) instead of your organization's default. See [Voting rules and quorum](/board/voting/rules-and-quorum/).

## Take notes

During the meeting, record notes against each agenda item. Notes feed into the minutes; see [Minutes](/board/records/minutes/).

## Record a motion

On the **Motions & votes** tab, **Record a motion** with its wording and the agenda item it belongs to (or **General / not tied to an item**). Then either hold a [live vote](/board/voting/live-voting/), or record the result directly (for example by voice vote).

Motion results: **Pending**, **Carried**, **Failed**, **Tabled** or **Withdrawn**.

## Locked after approval

Once the meeting's minutes are approved, its attendance and motions are part of the official record and can't be edited.
