**Settings** is for owners and administrators.

| Section | What you set | More |
|---|---|---|
| **Organization** | Name and **time zone** (used for meeting times and dates on records) | — |
| **Live votes** | Quorum, decision rule, how votes close, who closes them, and how votes are recorded | [Voting rules and quorum](/board/voting/rules-and-quorum/) |
| **Organization letterhead** | Stationery for minutes and packets | [Letterhead](/board/records/letterhead/) |
| **Data export** | Table exports and the full board record ZIP | [Exports](/board/records/exports/) |
| **Transfer ownership** | Hand the organization to another member | Below |

## Transferring ownership

Each organization has exactly one **Owner**. To hand over, for example when the chair changes:

1. **Settings → Transfer ownership.**
2. Choose the **new owner** from active members.
3. Type the organization's name to confirm.

The new person becomes Owner, and you become an Administrator, in one step. Only the current owner can do this.
