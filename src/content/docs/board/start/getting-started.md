## 1. Create an account and an organization

1. Open OpenBoardApp and choose sign in. Create an account with email and password, or use Google.
2. Create your organization: its name and time zone.

You're the organization's **Owner**.

## 2. Find your way around

The app lives under **Board** once you're signed in. The sidebar has:

| Page | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Upcoming meetings, minutes awaiting approval, and your action items, including overdue ones |
| **Meetings** | Schedule meetings and open each one's agenda, attendance, motions, minutes, packet and action items |
| **Members** | Who has access, their app roles, board roles and terms, and invitations |
| **Committees** | Committees and who's on them |
| **Documents** | The private document library |
| **Tasks** | Your action items |
| **Templates** | Meeting templates with standard agendas |
| **Audit** | Who changed what and when |
| **Settings** | Organization, voting rules, letterhead, data export and ownership |

If you belong to more than one organization, switch between them in the sidebar.

## 3. Set up your board

1. **Settings:** check the time zone, set your **quorum** and your **voting rules**. See [Voting rules and quorum](/board/voting/rules-and-quorum/).
2. **Members → Board roles & terms:** create roles such as Chair, Secretary and Treasurer with their term lengths. See [Board roles and terms](/board/people/board-roles-and-terms/).
3. **Members → Invite someone:** invite each board member with the right app role. See [Inviting people](/board/people/inviting/).
4. **Settings → Organization letterhead** (optional): upload your stationery for minutes and packets.
5. **Templates** (optional): create a standard agenda for regular meetings.

## 4. Hold your first meeting

[Schedule a meeting](/board/meetings/scheduling/), build the agenda, take attendance on the day, vote live, then write and approve the minutes.

## Try the demo

A demo organization, "Riverside Community Association", is available with sample meetings, minutes and votes. On the sign-in page, press **Explore the demo board**. You're signed in as Alex Morgan, the demo board's Vice President, with administrator access. The demo resets itself every six hours, so feel free to change anything.
