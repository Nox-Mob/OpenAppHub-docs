Live voting lets board members vote from their own phone or laptop during a meeting, and saves an official record of the result that can't be changed afterwards.

## Before the meeting

- Set your [voting rules and quorum](/board/voting/rules-and-quorum/) in Settings.
- Make sure every voting member has accepted their invitation, so their account can be linked to their attendance.
- Mark vote items on the agenda.

## During the meeting

1. Set the meeting to **In progress**. Votes can only open and be cast while a meeting is in progress.
2. Take [attendance](/board/meetings/attendance/). Members present or remote, with a linked account, are eligible.
3. Record the motion, then press **Open a vote**.
4. Members see the vote on the **live meeting dashboard** and choose **Yes**, **No**, **Abstain** or **Recuse**.
5. Each member confirms: **Confirm your official vote**. A vote can't be changed once cast.
6. The vote closes according to your settings, and the result is recorded.

## Who can vote

The **eligible voters are frozen** when the vote opens: everyone present or remote with a linked account at that moment. Someone who arrives after the vote opens can't vote on it, and someone who leaves can still have their vote counted. This prevents arguments about who could vote.

Each person can vote once. The database enforces this, not only the screen.

## Only one vote at a time

A meeting can have only one open vote at a time. Close it before opening the next.

## How votes close

Set in Settings:

- **The chair (or another permitted role) closes it**
- **Closes automatically once every eligible member has voted**
- **Closes when a timer runs out**

## Abstain versus recuse

- **Abstain:** you take part but don't vote yes or no.
- **Recuse:** you don't take part in this decision at all, for example because of a conflict of interest. Recused members are left out of the count when the rule is "majority of those present".

## After the vote

The result and full details are saved on the motion. See [The official vote record](/board/voting/official-record/).

## Votes without the app

For a voice vote or show of hands, record the motion's result directly instead of opening a live vote.
