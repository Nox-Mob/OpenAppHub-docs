Owners and administrators set these in **Settings**. Check them against your by-laws.

## Quorum

**Quorum (members required):** the number of board members who must be present for a decision. The live quorum indicator in meetings uses it.

## Decision rule for live votes

| Rule | A motion carries when… |
|---|---|
| **Simple majority of votes cast** | Yes votes are more than No votes. Abstentions don't count. |
| **Majority of the eligible voters present** | Yes votes are more than half of the eligible voters present, not counting recused members. Abstaining counts like a No. |
| **Two-thirds of votes cast** | Yes votes are at least two-thirds of Yes plus No. |
| **Custom: the chair records the result** | The app counts the votes, and the chair records the outcome with an explanation. |

### A different rule for one item

A vote item on the agenda can have its own **decision rule**, for example two-thirds to amend the by-laws. Leave it blank to use the organization's default.

The rule in force is captured **when the vote opens**. Changing settings later doesn't change a vote already taken.

## How live votes close

- the chair (or another permitted role) closes it
- automatically, once every eligible member has voted
- when a timer runs out (**Timer (minutes)**)

**Who can close a vote and record results:** choose which roles may do this.

## How votes are recorded

- **Record how each board member voted:** the official record lists each person's vote.
- **Vote totals only (roll call optional per motion):** the record shows totals; you can still choose a roll call for a particular motion.

Check your by-laws and local law on whether individual votes must be recorded.
