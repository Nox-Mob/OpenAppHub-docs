## Recording a motion

On a meeting's **Motions & votes** tab, record each motion against its **Agenda item**:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Motion** | The text, for example *Move to adopt the 2027 budget as presented* |
| **Moved by**, **Seconded by** | Board members. If **Motions require a seconder** is on, a motion can't go to a vote without one |
| **Recusals** | Members who step out of this vote (for example because of a conflict of interest); they aren't counted |
| **Outcome** | **Carried**, **Failed**, **Tabled** or **Withdrawn**, with the counts or each member's vote |

## Voting live

Members can also vote from their own phone or computer during the meeting. The chair (or another permitted role) starts a vote from the agenda item; each eligible member sees it, chooses **Yes**, **No**, **Abstain** or **Recuse**, and confirms that it's their official vote. Results update as votes come in, and the vote closes as your settings say. The full record (who was eligible, who voted and how, when it closed) is kept with the meeting and goes into the minutes.

Eligible voters are the board members with a current term on the meeting's date ([Members and terms](/board/people/members-and-terms/)).

## Voting settings

Owners and admins set these under **Settings & export**:

| Setting | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Quorum (members required)** | How many board members must be present for the meeting to make decisions, as your bylaws say. **Attendance** shows whether quorum is met |
| **How votes are recorded** | **Vote totals only** (roll call optional per motion), or **Record how each board member voted** by name. Some bylaws and public bodies require names |
| **Decision rule for live votes** | How many yes votes a motion needs to pass (below). A single agenda item can use a different rule |
| **Motions require a seconder** | When on, a motion can't go to a vote until another member seconds it |
| **How live votes close** | One or more: **the chair (or another permitted role) closes it**, **closes automatically once every eligible member has voted**, **closes when a timer runs out** (with the **Timer (minutes)**). A vote closes as soon as any chosen condition is met |

### Decision rules

| Rule | A motion passes when |
|---|---|
| **Simple majority of votes cast** | More yes than no votes; abstentions don't count |
| **Majority of the eligible voters present** | Yes votes are more than half of the eligible voters (not counting anyone who recused), so abstaining or not voting works like voting no |
| **Two-thirds of votes cast** | Yes votes are at least two-thirds of the yes and no votes |
| **Custom: the chair records the result** | The app doesn't calculate it: when the vote closes, the chair (or another permitted role) records **Carried** or **Failed**, for rules the app doesn't know (for example a supermajority of the full board) |

When a live vote closes, OpenBoardApp calculates the result with the rule. If **quorum wasn't met**, or the rule is custom, nobody is declared the winner automatically: a permitted role records the result, with an explanation that's kept in the record. A calculated result can still be marked **Tabled** or **Withdrawn**.

## After the meeting

Once the minutes are approved, a meeting's motions and attendance are locked as part of the official record ([Minutes and documents](/board/records/minutes-and-documents/)).
