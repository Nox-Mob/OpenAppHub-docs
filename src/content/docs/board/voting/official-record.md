When a live vote closes, the app saves a permanent record on the motion. It's part of the official record and can't be edited.

## What's recorded

- when the vote **opened** and **closed**
- the **eligible voters** (frozen at opening)
- the counts: yes, no, abstained, **recused** and **did not vote**
- how each member voted, if your settings record individual votes
- whether **quorum** was met
- the **decision rule** that applied
- the **result**

## When the app can't decide by itself

Sometimes the app shouldn't invent a result:

- **quorum wasn't met**,
- **nobody voted yes or no** (for example, everyone abstained), or
- the rule is **Custom: the chair records the result**.

Then an authorized person uses **Record the result**, choosing the outcome and writing an **explanation (required)**. The explanation is saved with the record.

## Motion outcomes

**Carried**, **Failed**, **Tabled** or **Withdrawn**. A motion with no result yet is **Pending**.

## Why it can't be changed

Ballots and the list of eligible voters can't be edited or deleted by anyone, and the database blocks changes to a closed vote. If a decision needs revisiting, make a new motion at a later meeting.

## Where it appears

- the meeting's **Motions & votes** tab (**Votes taken in this meeting** on the live dashboard)
- the approved **minutes**
- the **board record ZIP** export
