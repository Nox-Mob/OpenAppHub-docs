**Committees** keeps a list of your board's committees.

## Add a committee

**Add committee**: enter the **committee name** and an optional **purpose**.

## Add members

In the committee, add each person by **name** with their **role** on the committee (for example Chair). Committee members don't need an account.

## Committee meetings

Committee meetings can be scheduled as meetings like any other, with their own agenda and minutes. Name them clearly (for example "Finance Committee, March") so they're easy to find.
