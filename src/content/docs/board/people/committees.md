**Committees** lists your organization's committees (finance, nominating, events…) and who serves on each, so everyone can see who to ask.

Owners, administrators, the chair and the secretary can add and change committees. Everyone on the board can see them.

## Adding a committee

1. Select **Committees** in the side menu.
2. Type the **Committee name** and, optionally, its **Purpose**, such as _Reviews the budget before each board meeting_.
3. Select **Add committee**.

## Adding people

Under a committee, type the person's **Name** and their **Role** (_Chair_, _Member_, _Secretary_…; _Member_ if left empty) and select add. Committee members don't need an account: anyone can be listed, including people who aren't on the board.

Remove someone with the remove button next to their name. **Delete committee** removes the committee and its list.

## Good to know

- Committee lists are a record of who serves. They don't give anyone permissions in OpenBoardApp; roles do ([Members and terms](/board/people/members-and-terms/)).
- Committee meetings aren't separate meetings in OpenBoardApp yet; record a committee's report as an agenda item of the board meeting.
