**Members → Board roles & terms** records your board's offices and who holds them over time.

## Create a board role

**Create a board role**: give it a **title** (for example Chair, Treasurer, Membership VP) and a **term length**, or **No fixed term**.

## Assign someone

Choose the role and the person. Their current title appears next to their name throughout the app, for example "Sam Lee — Treasurer".

## Handing over an office

When you assign a new holder, the previous holder's term ends the **day before** the new one starts. Nothing is overwritten: the role's full holder history is kept.

## Vacant roles

A role with no current holder shows **Vacant**.

## Older terms

**Older terms not tied to a role** lists historical terms recorded before roles were set up.

## Titles in documents

Minutes and board packets show each person's title **as of the meeting date**, so a packet from last year shows last year's officers.

## Board titles aren't app permissions

Being "Treasurer" here doesn't change what someone can do in the app. That's their [app role](/board/people/roles/).
