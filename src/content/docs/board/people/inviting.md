OpenBoardApp doesn't send email yet. You create an invitation link and send it yourself.

## Invite someone

1. **Members → Invite someone.**
2. Choose their **role**. Optionally enter their email for your records.
3. Copy the link: **Send this link (valid 14 days, single use)**.
4. Send it by email or message.

The person opens the link, signs in or creates an account, and accepts. They then appear under **App access**.

## Pending invites

**Members → Pending invites** lists links not yet accepted. Revoke any you no longer need.

## Removing someone

Owners and administrators can remove a member's access on the Members page. Their past attendance, votes and terms stay in the record. The owner can't be removed; transfer ownership first.

See [Invitations](/hub/accounts/invitations/) if a link doesn't work.
