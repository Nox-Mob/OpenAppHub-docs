Under **Members & terms**, keep the board roster, its roles (seats) and each person's term.

## Roles in OpenBoardApp

Everyone in your organization has one role in OpenBoardApp. Owners and admins set it on **Members & terms**; it changes the role in OpenBoardApp only, not in OpenAppHub or the other apps (to see or change everyone's role in every app, use **Roles in each app** on OpenAppHub's **Members** page: [Organizations and roles](/hub/accounts/organizations-and-roles/)).

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything, including transferring ownership |
| **Administrator** | Everything except transferring ownership: settings, inviting people, exports |
| **Chair**, **Secretary** | Edit the official record (meetings, agendas, attendance, motions, minutes, documents) and approve minutes; read the audit log |
| **Treasurer** | Read everything |
| **Board member** | Read everything, and vote in live votes they're eligible for |
| **Observer** | Read everything; never votes |

An officer role (chair, secretary, treasurer) sits on top of the person's OpenAppHub role (member); an observer is an OpenAppHub viewer. Inviting people happens in OpenAppHub ([Invitations](/hub/accounts/invitations/)).

## Board roles and terms

A **board role** is a seat on the board, like *President* or *Treasurer*. Under **Create a board role**, give it a **Title** and a **Term length**. Then **fill** it with a person and a start date:

- The role's current holder (if any) has their term ended the day before the new one starts, in the same step, so a role is never left with two holders or none by mistake.
- The new term's end date is set from the role's term length; you can change it.
- Past terms stay as the role's history.

Board members with a current term get [meeting reminders](/board/meetings/running-a-meeting/#reminders) and are the eligible voters in live votes.

## Committees

**Committees** lists your committees and their members, for example a finance or nominating committee.

## Officers from OpenCharterApp

If your organization also uses OpenCharterApp, its officers show here under **Officers from OpenCharterApp**.
Appoint or replace them in OpenCharterApp. You can end a term here (it ends in OpenCharterApp too), and a term you
add here for a position OpenCharterApp knows becomes an appointment there.
