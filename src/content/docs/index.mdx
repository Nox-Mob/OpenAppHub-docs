Welcome! These pages explain how to use the OpenAppHub apps. The apps are made for associations, boards and nonprofits. They're open source, which means anyone can see how they're built.

There are four apps. You sign in once, and you can use all of them. Pick the one you need help with.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="OpenAppHub" icon="puzzle">
    The home base. Your account, signing in, two-step sign-in, your
    organizations, plans and your data. [Open the OpenAppHub docs](/hub/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="OpenCharterApp" icon="open-book">
    Your members. Joining, renewing, chapters, dues and receipts. [Open the
    OpenCharterApp docs](/charter/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="OpenBoardApp" icon="comment">
    Your board. Meetings, agendas, votes, minutes and follow-up jobs. [Open the
    OpenBoardApp docs](/board/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="OpenLedgerApp" icon="document">
    Your money. Budgets, bookkeeping and reports. [Open the OpenLedgerApp
    docs](/ledger/)
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
